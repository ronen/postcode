# Proposed revisions to docs/capability-reference.md

Replace the paragraph beginning “The application submits observation batches” in “Presentation and operational facilities”.

The application submits observation batches to a separate local sink. New version-four batches record the actual normalized request independently of the produced View. Subject lookup reports preserve candidates and qualified outcomes when no answer can be selected. Equivalent names and references share retained answers. Session results and observations carry bounded request-scoped reports of prerequisite and evidence work. Captured-basis attribution remains incomplete. See [additional detail and observations](cli-reference.md#source-detail-and-observations), [request work attribution](architecture/README.md#request-work-attribution), and [basis attribution](backlog.md#preserve-captured-basis-attribution-in-observations). Do not infer complete research instrumentation from the existence of a sink. The current CLI does this automatically and has no public no-recording mode or question-specific capture control. This is current behavior, not a universal recording requirement: [research capture](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#research-capture) may be selective or absent in ordinary non-research use. Explicit feedback remains a deliberate submission. Optional decoupling is [backlogged](backlog.md#separate-optional-research-recording-from-feedback-context).
