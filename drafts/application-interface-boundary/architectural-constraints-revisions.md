## Application and interface boundary

- Own semantic request resolution, information requirements, Evaluation/investigation coordination, interpretation selection into answers and exact retained-answer access, reference validity and operational outcomes in shared application services. Interfaces invoke explicit internal contracts rather than reproducing those operations through store, selector or Evaluator access. The API is the request-validation boundary for direct callers. Keep application/domain dependencies independent of interface adapters; clients may choose which supplied answer to display. [[Shared application boundary](decisions/internal-application-api.md#shared-application-boundary)]
- Deliver selected qualified content and operational outcomes independently of encoded or bounded interface output. Current eager families deliver their full selected materialized content without prescribing universal eager materialization or exhaustive analysis. Preserve support, qualification, failure distinctions and answer identity separately from invocation reporting. Use precise IDs as the client contract; do not expose a general queryable store. Internal source-bearing support is not automatically human output or authorized external machine evidence. [[Qualified content delivery](decisions/internal-application-api.md#qualified-content-delivery)]
- Distinguish information requests, exact retained-answer access and operational reports. Exact access follows fixed retained support without reselection, Evaluation or new evidence; preserve historical outcomes, qualification and revision/association snapshots under the host-declared Session lifetime. [[Qualified content delivery](decisions/internal-application-api.md#qualified-content-delivery)]
- Keep input syntax, interface defaults, Presentation options, arrangement and output presentation in clients. Capture client-owned declarations once at composition or Session opening, with application validation of semantic requirements; requests select among that captured set. Direct information requests default to current subject-kind standard expansions for the answer family, with supported subsets explicit; reconsider the default for future costly expansions. Presentation requirements do not authorize arrangement to acquire information or silently change the question or selected answer. [[Requirements and Presentations](decisions/internal-application-api.md#requirements-and-presentations)]
- Preserve append-only alias infrastructure and existing coordination allocations. Resolve accepted input aliases in shared coordination, preserving supplied designations and refusal distinctions. Where an interface uses compact aliases, confine arrangement-time binding to its supplied population within the serialized requesting operation; revoke that capability on settlement, invalidation or close. The decision limits this access to the current CLI; this invariant does not introduce a general binding API. Do not require general clients to allocate compact aliases. Keep completed application work distinct from failed client continuation presentation; preserve the interface's failure outcome. [[CLI binding and outcomes](decisions/internal-application-api.md#cli-bindings-and-outcomes)]
- Preserve resource ownership, typed lifecycle outcomes, progress/usage, credentials, guarded publication validity and actual-display feedback across the boundary, independently of research capture. Keep the batch envelope/sink/acknowledgment neutral and CLI observation content/classification with its Views. Deliver plain structured-clone-safe data without store handles or executable capabilities; future human clients must verify their output disclosure. This establishes no public transport or compatibility commitment. [[Runtime and publication](decisions/internal-application-api.md#runtime-and-publication)]
