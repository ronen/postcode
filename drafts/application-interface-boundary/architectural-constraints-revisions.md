## Application and interface boundary

- Own semantic validation, qualified resolution, information requirements, Evaluation/investigation, answer selection, exact retained access and application lifecycle in shared services behind explicit internal contracts. Clients do not reproduce those operations through stores, selectors or Evaluators. Application/domain dependencies must not reach interface adapters. [[Shared boundary](decisions/internal-application-api.md#shared-application-boundary)]
- Deliver selected qualified content independently of bounded or encoded output. Preserve support, qualification and failure distinctions. Current eager families deliver their full selected content without imposing universal eager materialization or exhaustive analysis. Precise IDs suffice; no general queryable store is exposed. Internal source-bearing support is not automatically human output or authorized external evidence. [[Qualified delivery](decisions/internal-application-api.md#qualified-content-delivery)]
- Distinguish information requests, exact retained access and operational reports. Exact access follows fixed support without reselection, Evaluation or new evidence, preserving historical outcomes and revision/association snapshots within the host-declared lifetime. [[Exact access](decisions/internal-application-api.md#qualified-content-delivery)]
- Keep interface syntax, defaults, Presentation policy and output in clients; shared coordination validates captured requirements and performs compatibility/refusal decisions. Direct content requirements need no Presentation and cannot require expansions outside their content contract. Arrangement cannot acquire information or broaden an answer. [[Requirements and Presentations](decisions/internal-application-api.md#requirements-and-presentations)]
- Preserve typed application attribution separately from optional client invocation context and answer identity. Completed application work is not retroactively failed by subsequent client presentation or publication failure; keep those outcomes distinct without hiding either. [[Context](decisions/internal-application-api.md#invocation-context)] [[Outcomes](decisions/internal-application-api.md#cli-bindings-and-outcomes)]
- Preserve append-only aliases, accepted input-alias resolution and established population-wide coordination. Confine arrangement-time binding to supplied reference populations within the serialized requesting operation, revoking capability on settlement, invalidation or close. Do not require other clients to allocate compact aliases or expose a general binding API. [[Reference coordination](decisions/internal-application-api.md#cli-bindings-and-outcomes)]
- Keep client Presentation-method versions out of application input and Session method capture. Record responsible methods on the artifacts they produce; a renderer-only change must not change application answer identity. [[Method provenance](decisions/internal-application-api.md#method-provenance)]
- Preserve ownership, typed lifecycle outcomes, progress/usage, credentials and guarded publication validity across the boundary independently of research capture. Shared delivery/feedback contracts must not depend on interface View types. Deliver plain data without store handles or executable capabilities; human clients must verify their actual output disclosure. This establishes no public transport or compatibility commitment. [[Runtime and publication](decisions/internal-application-api.md#runtime-and-publication)]
