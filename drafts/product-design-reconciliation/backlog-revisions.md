## Establish source-language-independent human representations

Added: 2026-10-29
Origin: [Human-facing architectural representations](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#adoption-and-conformance)
Area: human understanding, evidence detail, generated explanations and diagnostics

The adopted premise requires human understanding not to depend on programming-language source or syntax. The explicit syntax-oriented escape needs reconciliation. Source origin, free-form prose or lack of a scrubber alone does not establish a defect; intelligible assertions, labels, evidence and diagnostics should remain with provenance and epistemological status preserved.

Use the [reconciliation assessment](plans/product-design-reconciliation.md) to scope a bounded detail-change task and proportionate assessment of other channels. Agree whether to narrow, rename or remove `--source-detail`; preserve useful evidence detail, exact retained answers, source acquisition and agent access. Address fresh/retained, text/JSON and embedded presentations and their effective-choice continuations. That task need not implement an account framework or generic text gate, and does not alone establish product-wide semantic adequacy.

Assess representative documentation, generated explanations, qualifications, correction text and diagnostics for actual dependence on source-language literacy. Explain or disclose demonstrated gaps; preserve independently intelligible information rather than rejecting entire outputs on source origin. Update investigator guidance for the intended audience and choose further measures according to observed failures. Rendering supplied meaning is presentation; inferring a new contract or explanation requires qualified analysis. Verification must include preservation of useful source-derived content as well as failure cases involving code deciphering, with scope and limitations stated. A syntax scan or universal classifier is not required.

Prioritize replacing the incompatible escape and remedying demonstrated gaps before claiming those interactions satisfy the intended premise. Keep actual-output observations truthful and historical records unchanged. Coordinate affected tests, identity methods, compatibility and descriptive documentation with implementation; no implementation is authorized by this entry.

## Provide representation-limit feedback

Added: 2026-10-29
Origin: [Representation limits and feedback](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#representation-limits-and-feedback)
Area: human interaction and research observation

Implement explicit representation-limit disclosure and low-friction optional inadequacy reporting for the supported human interface. Generic analysis failures and source-escape observations are not this interaction. Preserve available View/exact answer, subject, captured basis and investigation context; distinguish absent context and the supported lifetime rather than reconstructing it from current state or promising persistence. Feedback is experience evidence, not a correction or program-change request. Do not block continuation on submitting it.

Choose concrete controls and retention/delivery behavior through bounded UX design. This is required application behavior with delivery still to be scoped, independent of selecting an architectural-account schema. Use the existing observation boundary and non-blocking delivery-failure contract. The separate captured-basis attribution and navigation-origin backlog entries remain relevant dependencies for claims of complete contextual recording; do not claim they are resolved by adding a feedback button or command.

## Explore architectural accounts through concrete journeys

Added: 2026-10-29
Origin: [Allow structured investigram content](decisions/structured-investigram-content.md)
Area: architectural representations and retained interpretation

Explore structured architectural accounts carried as investigram content, potentially with several selected investigrams forming a working account. This is a promising approach, not an architectural decision to adopt it. Begin with concrete journeys and selected representations—such as operation admission/cancellation or responsibility across a boundary—and discover the content each needs. Evaluate usefulness to an architect without source-language literacy, not merely successful rendering or schema validity.

Keep partiality, conflicts, evidence, revision history and interpretive status attributable. Reuse retention, associations and replacement mechanisms where they fit; a selected account set need not be one mutable composition tree or a canonical architecture. Defer account schemas, selection, correspondence and subclaim identity until the journeys establish the need. Before extending beyond an unchanged-input Session, resolve lifecycle, captured-basis and reference/correspondence choices. Use the existing coordinated-View, navigation and coding-agent context candidates rather than building parallel infrastructure by assumption. This capability exploration is distinct from required semantic representation conformance and authorizes no implementation.

## Assess attribution disclosure outside explicit detail fields

Added: 2026-09-30
Origin: [Module investigation milestone-2 round-3 review](../records/reviews/module-investigation/2026-09-29-milestone-2-round-3-findings.md); reconciled by [Human-facing architectural representations](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md)
Area: human representation, observation semantics and implementation provenance

The earlier review left paths outside `sourceDetail`, including configuration paths in JSON analysis context, unassessed under the then-current source-excursion policy. Preserve that historical finding. The clarified policy permits intelligible source-derived assertions, labels and evidence while requiring understanding independent of source-language literacy.

Assess useful attribution disclosure and its recording across relevant text/JSON and non-View results as part of the scoped human-representation work. Distinguish human-visible evidence detail from machine/internal context; do not classify a path or source-origin string as prohibited simply by origin. Actual-detail metadata can remain useful without treating access as inadequacy or syntax classification as proof of conformance. This replaces the old question of missing source-escape events; it does not claim the assessment is complete or require wholesale observation schema replacement or historical migration.
