# Proposed revisions to docs/core-concepts.md

## Investigram: replace the term-level decision link and first definition paragraph only

*[decision: [Allow structured investigram content](decisions/structured-investigram-content.md#permit-structured-interpretation)]*

An **Investigram** is an immutable, addressable artifact of program investigation containing a qualified interpretation, expressed in prose, structured content, or both, with referent information, evidence context and provenance. Its provenance identifies the operation and selected subject that produced it. It may contain multiple related claims. Structured form does not make its content mechanically established or require a consolidated architecture model.

Retain the subsequent referent, association, composition, correction and citation paragraphs and their localized provenance links unchanged. The new decision explicitly preserves those meanings while generalizing complete context to the applicable content.

## Evidence: append to the second paragraph

Source acquisition and agent access are distinct from human presentation. Human-facing attribution, intelligible source-derived assertions and implementation mappings preserve provenance and epistemological status without requiring source-language literacy; captured source can remain available through defined machine evidence interfaces. [[Human output boundary](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#human-output-boundary)]

## Expansion: replace its second paragraph

Standard expansions concern the subject kind's presentation semantics, independently of the Lens that selected the subject. Supporting implementation detail can include evidence attribution and mappings; human-facing expansions preserve intelligible meaning without depending on source-language literacy. Assertions, labels and evidence from source artifacts remain eligible when their provenance and epistemological status are preserved. Source-bearing evidence can remain available internally and to agents through their defined access interfaces. [[Human output boundary](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#human-output-boundary)] Exposing another Lens's answer may add its Projection to a coordinating Presentation or embed another View. Requesting that answer goes through Evaluation and core construction; presenting an already supplied result does not require reevaluation. Inline placement alone does not make an additional Lens question a standard expansion. Dependency children and dependency parents, for example, remain separately meaningful Lens questions rather than standard module expansions. [[Coordinated expansion](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#keep-evaluation-and-qualified-construction-independent-of-presentation)]

No new Working Account, Architectural Entity or Account Schema core term is introduced. Existing Subject, Claim, Projection, Presentation/View, investigation context, Session and Workspace definitions suffice for this reconciliation.
