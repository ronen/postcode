# Reconcile the application with the architectural working premise

Status: in review
Created: 2026-10-29
Updated: 2026-11-02

## Purpose and scope

Humans practice software design and architecture through PostCode; coding agents implement the software. The [adopted product design](../../foundation/product-design.md) presumes computational and architectural understanding without programming-language literacy. Human understanding must not depend on programming-language source or syntax. This is a semantic restriction: intelligible assertions, labels and evidence from source artifacts may be presented with their provenance and epistemological status preserved. A code excerpt the human must decipher does not answer an architectural question.

Reconcile the application specification, governing architecture and still-binding decisions with that premise, representation-limit feedback and partial qualified architectural accounts. Adoption establishes intended contracts and identifies follow-up work; it implements nothing. No foundation or development-instruction revision, account schema, promotion or implementation authorization is included.

## Suitable concepts and mechanisms


Retain Lens/Projection/Presentation/View, open-ended Subject, Claim and Claim context, source Evidence, Facet, Evaluation, Session, Workspace and investigation context. The conceptual/implementation distinction is useful; “language-specific” does not mean “unfit for human reasoning.” A compiler may establish a property whose meaning can be conveyed architecturally. Conversely, relabelling a raw signature as a contract does not make it an architectural representation.

Keep immutable evidence/results, exact references, captured support, explicit associations, fixed result composition, follow-up provenance, replacement history, conflicting corrections and citation-based reconsideration. Existing accounts are useful qualified interpretation, not a complete architecture. Qualification cannot be hidden to make a view simpler. Read-only Views and human instructions to coding agents remain the development model.

Keep qualified construction separate from arrangement. New architectural synthesis or inferred correspondence requires analysis and a qualified Projection; selecting or arranging supplied accounts need not manufacture new claims. Keep generated outputs distinct from independent repository evidence. An earlier account can be reused as interpretation without its repetition corroborating itself.

It does not alone establish semantic adequacy across the product. Broader work should be staged according to demonstrated representation gaps, not organized as an exhaustive source-text scrubbing project.

These are an implementation inventory and scoped assessment findings, not a runtime certification. The explicit source-escape purpose conflicts with the adopted working premise. Arbitrary text channels require assessment of their actual content and use; their existence does not justify declaring every output nonconforming. No automatic classifier can replace the question of whether the representation supports architectural understanding.

### Bounded follow-up and stage criteria

1. **Rework the explicit escape and preserve useful detail.** Agree the user-visible treatment of `--source-detail` and its continuations. Keep intelligible evidence, assertions, provenance and mappings; remove or rework the route whose intended use requires reading source syntax. Cover fresh/retained, text/JSON and embedded families. Exit: the supported interaction no longer offers code deciphering as the answer to a representation limit, useful qualified detail remains available, and exact answers/agent evidence access are preserved. This can be separately scoped without an account framework or generic text gate.
2. **Assess and remedy concrete semantic gaps.** Use representative investigations, documentation, generated explanations and diagnostics to identify where meaning depends on source-language literacy. Include referent/qualification/correction text and failure paths where relevant. Update investigator guidance and address demonstrated defects with targeted measures. Exit: the selected journeys and failure cases are intelligible at the presumed level, their qualifications survive and remaining unsupported cases are disclosed. Scope broader changes only when evidence shows a common problem. Passing a syntax scan or removing all excerpts is not an exit criterion.
3. **Provide representation-limit feedback.** Offer low-friction optional reports tied to available exact View/answer, subject, capture and investigation context. Distinguish experience feedback from program truth, correction or authorization to change software. Verify observation accuracy and delivery failure behavior. Feedback design can proceed alongside other work and need not wait for structured accounts or a GUI.

These are separately authorizable outcomes, not a delivery schedule. A supported subset can be useful while disclosing its limits. If available content cannot meet a need without code deciphering, say what remains unresolved, preserve independently intelligible information and offer further qualified investigation where appropriate. Refuse a particular inadequate representation when necessary; do not default to whole-result rejection or suppress useful text merely because it originated in source.

### Verification criteria

Use positive and negative cases together. Positive cases include intelligible in-source documentation with recorded-assertion status, labels and provenance, qualified evidence quotations, and useful compiler/provider diagnostics. Preserve these even if a syntax-oriented heuristic would flag their spelling or origin. Negative cases include a raw signature substituted for an explanation of a contract, an unexplained expression used to justify behavior, and diagnostics whose meaning or required action depends on parsing the implementation language. A confident prose gloss without sufficient evidence also fails.

Verify that formatting or displaying supplied meaning does not silently add a claim. A rendering of already-established semantic information is presentation; inferring a responsibility or contract from raw source is analysis with its own qualification. Exact `present` must keep its frozen selection and support; it can show an intelligible assertion already in that support or disclose a representation limit, but cannot silently regenerate an explanation. Test fresh/retained and direct/worker delivery, public text/JSON, relevant refusal/stderr paths and preserved machine evidence access with existing fixtures and investigator doubles. Use human journey review at the presumed architectural competence for usefulness and semantic adequacy; record the assessed scope rather than claiming exhaustive correctness of unrestricted prose.

## Architectural accounts: remove the obstacle, leave the design open


Structured accounts carried as investigram content are a promising hypothesis. Existing retention, evidence, associations and explicit replacements give it a plausible home. A working account might comprise several selected investigrams, with initial content shapes driven by selected architectural representations. That selection need not be one investigram, a new Entity or a mutable composition tree.

The prose-only definition is an unnecessary restriction; the companion decision defines [interpretation content](../core-concepts.md#interpretation-content) independently of representation and applies the existing contracts to all of that content. The requirement that investigrams remain interpretive is suitable: structured claims do not become mechanical facts. Mechanically established information can remain separate qualified evidence/Projections. Fixed composition is also suitable: it describes one produced result, not every grouping humans may select later. The existing choice not to require a canonical architecture model does not prohibit partial models or accounts.

Other contracts impose useful obligations, not blockers: typed result/Presentation compatibility; immutable references and replacements; evidence exposure and complete correction context; generated-output separation; qualified relationships between interpretations and implementation. Correction eligibility requires complete retained interpretation content, referent information and qualifications. Complete correction-context delivery also covers the replacement's own content and the correction reasons and qualifications; the existing subordinate-tree exemption remains. A structured content implementation must realize these same contracts and extend the mechanisms where necessary. Current prose fields, validation, context delivery, paging and rendering are implementation limits, not proof that the domain needs a new account store.

Leave schemas, selected-account semantics, correspondence, representation choice, editing, independent subclaim identity and cross-session persistence to subsequent work grounded in journeys. A same-Session experiment need not solve cross-state identity. Reuse across changed captures or restarts does require separate lifecycle, basis and correspondence decisions. “Across investigations” does not inherently mean “persist across sessions,” nor does a selected set establish a complete or internally consistent architecture.

Candidate journeys for that later design: understand how an operation is admitted and cancelled; inspect responsibility and ownership across a boundary; ask an agent to change that boundary and evaluate the resulting behavior. Use these to discover which account structure is needed rather than imposing a general schema now. Do not implement or prioritize these capabilities through this reconciliation.
