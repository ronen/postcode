## Source evidence and human understanding

- Human understanding must not depend on programming-language source or syntax. Apply this to Views, generated explanations, evidence detail, qualifications, diagnostics and non-View reports. A code excerpt, declaration or signature that must be deciphered does not satisfy an architectural need. Human use of public CLI text or JSON has the same semantic requirement; encoding does not establish audience. [[Human representation boundary](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#human-output-boundary)]
- Assertions, labels and evidence embedded in source artifacts may be presented when intelligible without source-language literacy, preserving provenance and epistemological status. Implementation identifiers, paths, references and mappings may support traceability. Do not suppress useful information solely because it originated in source or resembles code, or hide material qualifications to simplify it. [[Human representation boundary](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#human-output-boundary)]
- Preserve source acquisition, captured evidence and implementation mappings for analysis and authorized agent access. Evidence attributed or delivered for a claim comes from its supporting capture, not a later filesystem read. Machine evidence delivery is distinct from human exposure; existing investigator access restrictions remain in force. [[Captured evidence](decisions/adopt-identity-evidence-and-observation-constraints.md#present-source-evidence-from-captured-analysis-input)] [[Human representation boundary](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#human-output-boundary)]
- When available representation is inadequate, disclose the limitation and retain independently intelligible qualified information. Omit or refuse the inadequate representation when necessary, not simply because of source origin. Rendering supplied meaning remains presentation; new architectural inference requires qualified analysis and must not silently occur inside arrangement or refresh an exact retained answer. [[Representation limits](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#representation-limits-and-feedback)]
- Preserve actual human output, representation-limit disclosures and submitted inadequacy feedback with available View/answer, subject, captured basis and investigation context. Feedback reports experience, not program truth or a correction. Evidence-detail access does not establish inadequacy; machine acquisition is not human source escape. Preserve useful exposure metadata and historical observations without treating syntax classification as a semantic adequacy test. [[Representation limits and feedback](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#representation-limits-and-feedback)]

## In “Retained interpretation,” add after the existing constraints

- Apply immutable retention, qualification, evidence attribution, qualified associations, exact references, composition, corrections and citation exposure rules to all [interpretation content](core-concepts.md#interpretation-content), regardless of representation. Structural validity does not establish truth. Correction eligibility requires the target's complete retained interpretation content, referent information and qualifications supplied during the current evaluation. Complete correction-context delivery requires both the target's and replacement's own interpretation content, referent information and qualifications, together with correction reasons and qualifications; it does not require their subordinate investigram trees. References, summaries and partial delivery do not establish completeness. This does not require implementing a structured account schema. [[Structured interpretation](decisions/structured-investigram-content.md#permit-structured-interpretation)]

Existing exact-retained-presentation and qualified-construction constraints stay in force. Their source-escape commitments are reconciled through the new decision's scoped supersession mappings, not by rewriting historical records or suppressing intelligible evidence.
