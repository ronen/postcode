# Proposed revisions to docs/capability-reference.md

## Add after the introductory paragraph beginning “PostCode currently provides”

The adopted design supports humans practicing software architecture without programming-language literacy. Intelligible assertions, labels and evidence from source artifacts remain useful when provenance and epistemological status are preserved. The CLI's syntax-oriented source escape needs reconciliation; it does not supply an architectural account merely by exposing code. Other prose and diagnostic paths need assessment of actual semantic adequacy, not blanket scrubbing based on origin. See the [reconciliation assessment](plans/product-design-reconciliation.md) and [human-representation follow-up](backlog.md#establish-source-language-independent-human-representations). There is no dedicated implemented inadequacy-feedback interaction; [representation-limit feedback](backlog.md#provide-representation-limit-feedback) remains to be scoped and delivered.

Exact retained-answer presentation is implemented within an active Session. Partial qualified interpretation can accumulate through investigrams and follow-up investigations, but structured architectural accounts, general working-account selection and cross-session persistence are not implemented capabilities. Permission to explore them does not select their architecture.
