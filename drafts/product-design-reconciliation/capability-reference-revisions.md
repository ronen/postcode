# Proposed revisions to docs/capability-reference.md

Delivery note: these descriptions accompany the required implementation and must be verified against delivered behavior before publication as current capabilities. The [assessed baseline](plans/product-design-reconciliation.md#assessed-baseline) records what exists before that work. The implementation task also updates the CLI reference with the chosen feedback syntax and actual supported options.

## Replace the introductory paragraph beginning “PostCode currently provides”

PostCode provides a development CLI. Investigation operates over one configured TypeScript project, and the interactive shell retains an investigation within one process. Standalone inadequacy feedback does not require an investigation context. There is no implemented GUI Workspace, general multi-Projection View composition, multi-state analysis, or save/resume facility. The default command is `modules`; `summarize` is an explicit request for interpretation. Retained prose interpretations and follow-up investigations provide mechanisms for accumulating understanding; structured architectural accounts and the broader accumulation UX remain unresolved design work.

## In “Presentation and operational facilities,” replace the first paragraph

Unicode text and experimental JSON are human-facing presentation formats. Shell `present @answer-…` preserves an exact retained answer while changing format, supported additional detail or paging. Fresh Lens commands select anew and reject paging flags. `--more-details` exposes additional qualified information, attribution, locations and implementation mappings without source excerpts or declarations. Intelligible assertions, labels and evidence from source remain eligible with provenance and epistemological status preserved. Options depend on the retained content family; the CLI reference is authoritative for their syntax. Analysis and Investigator evidence access retain captured source independently of human presentation.

## In “Presentation and operational facilities,” add after the paragraph beginning “`usage` reports”

Inadequacy feedback accepts an optional explanation. In the shell it concerns the most recently displayed result and includes its available context. A one-shot feedback invocation has no associated prior-result or investigation context and requires no project opening or investigator enablement. Feedback records and submits the report without changing the investigation or initiating further work. Investigation can continue without waiting for delivery; delivery failure is disclosed without invalidating the displayed result or blocking continued use.

## In “Presentation and operational facilities,” replace the paragraph beginning “The application submits observation batches”

The application submits versioned experimental observation batches to a separate local sink. Investigation observations record the actual normalized request independently of the produced View. Subject lookup reports preserve candidates and qualified outcomes when no answer can be selected. Equivalent names and references share retained answers. Session results and observations carry bounded request-scoped reports of prerequisite and evidence work. Feedback records available context without fabricating a View, Session or captured basis. Detail access, source display, recognized representation limits and reported inadequacy remain distinct. Captured-basis attribution remains incomplete. See [additional detail and observations](cli-reference.md#source-detail-and-observations), [request work attribution](architecture/README.md#request-work-attribution), and [basis attribution](backlog.md#preserve-captured-basis-attribution-in-observations). Do not infer complete research instrumentation from the existence of a sink.
