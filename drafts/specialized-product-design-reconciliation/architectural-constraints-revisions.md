# Proposed revisions to docs/architectural-constraints.md

Add this section after Claims and qualification. Existing program-state rules remain binding in their scope; the additions extend the guarantees to architectural accounts without requiring implementation of new modalities.

## Architectural accounts and captured bases

- Distinguish implemented, hypothetical and intended architecture from provenance, method and epistemological status. Recording a proposal establishes neither feasibility, acceptance nor realization. Observed behavior remains execution-scoped evidence. Preserve provenance and transition history when a proposal becomes an intention; sharing an account does not establish participant agreement or PostCode authority. This rule requires no modality field or design-acceptance workflow in the current implemented-only subset. [[Architectural modalities](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#architectural-modalities)]
- Apply a Lens only where its method supports the subject and modality. New architectural claims, including correspondence, conformance, contradiction and synthesized explanation, require core analysis and a qualified result. Request interpretation and presentation cannot independently derive those claims. This extends the existing program-claim boundary, including its permissions for arranging and counting supplied qualified populations, without adding evidence access. [[Architectural scope](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#results-over-captured-bases)]
- Views and Projections do not directly modify the program or silently rewrite their underlying material. Participant contributions, where supported, remain distinguishable from earlier captures and from accepted intentions. Receiving material is not itself a change to the investigated program. [[Read-only investigation](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#specialized-product-role)]
- Retain earlier Projections or participant material as identifiable, qualified contributions when used in a captured basis. Preserve supporting associations, scope, provenance, limitations and meaningful roles or order. Do not turn retained interpretation into a captured program state or independent repository corroboration. Preserve the generated-output evidence boundary. These requirements do not prescribe generalized input capture or new following/pinned policies. [[Captured bases](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#inputs-and-captured-bases)]
- Preserve the meaning and provenance of entities introduced by hypothetical or intended accounts; correspondence to other accounts or implemented entities requires support and qualification. Addressability and matching labels alone establish no such correspondence. Existing implemented-state identity and Session rules remain binding. [[Architectural subjects](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#architectural-subjects)]
