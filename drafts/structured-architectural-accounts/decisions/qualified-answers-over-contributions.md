## Context

Subject, Lens, Projection, Presentation and View describe the application's investigative answers and their use. Investigations, contributions, associations and Query-result designations supply engine information underneath. Confusing these levels would turn retrieving or rendering an answer into new production, or make a later organizing judgment silently replace a mechanical method's finding.

The common model changes selection and standing without weakening qualified-answer identity, exact presentation, or the separation of analysis and presentation. Historical answer captures and current derived status are both useful; neither should masquerade as the other.

## Decisions

### Application answers and captured bases

A Lens defines the question, applicability/cardinality, parameters, requirements and prospective result contract. Its implementation selects the qualified information answering that question. A Projection captures that selection immutably, including subject, Lens, parameters, supporting basis/method, qualifications, relevant outcomes and answer-relevant standing. A Query-result designation can supply selection but is not a Projection. No one-to-one relationship among Lenses, invocations, investigrams and Projections is required.

A captured basis identifies the particular material used: program states, observations, participant-supplied architectural material, earlier Projections or other retained qualified contributions. Preserve attribution, scope, limitations, meaningful ordering/roles and associations between material and the information it supports. Capturing material establishes neither truth nor agreement. Program inputs designate sources; revision bindings retain following/pinned meaning. Captured program state is one possible basis contribution, not all bases. Participant material, retained contributions and selection snapshots do not acquire revision binding policies by analogy. A Session supplies analysis/reference context, not proof of an atomic or complete snapshot.

Use **context selection** for choosing qualified engine information and **context delivery** for actually supplying it to a producer. Reserve **Projection** for the outer qualified application answer.

Distinguish the material actually delivered to a producer from the material later selected into an answer. Production retains actual input delivery and source support. Answer construction captures selected content and relevant association/designation/status snapshots without changing that earlier production history. Neither is evidence independently corroborating the same repository merely because PostCode retained it. Supporting attribution can be distributed across exact immutable records rather than one manifest; IDs alone do not describe all delivered content or support.

**Answer assembly, retrieval and presentation create no production exposure.** Reading retained module naming to construct an export Projection is selection. If a producer consumes those names to establish new content or an organizing judgment, it records its real inputs. One application request can coordinate both activities, but synthesis or correspondence must not be hidden as assembly. Membership concerns and source-producer concerns remain accessible; lack of new exposure is not clearance of existing concerns.

Exposure, derivation provenance and amendment explain contribution ancestry, not a new application hierarchy or a total order of truth. The application can receive raw engine information or mapped application-level information, provided qualification, scope and exact traceability survive. Do not broaden a contribution-specific relation to an entire answer or infer reliance from exposure. No generic ancestry graph or acyclicity validator is required beyond each mechanism's reference rules.

Consumers must not have to infer missing relationships from chronology. Supply the relevant exact relationship meaning through the supported application access.

**Rationale.** The outer model remains useful independently of engine representation. Separating immutable selection from production avoids invented dependencies while preserving both captured evidence and current organizing history.

### Lens answers and engine selection

A Lens returns what its declared question and method ask for. A method-specific query continues to return that method's qualified findings, even if later associations withdraw or reinterpret their use. Complete applicable standing, displacement, cause and qualified history information remains available to the application through exact access. A query asking for the current retained answer instead selects current result designations, preserving all equally standing alternatives without hidden priority. This distinction follows question meaning, not a blanket mechanical/agentic rule.

When the last standing designation has been withdrawn, ordinary retrieval of the current answer reports an explicit **no current designated answer** condition, with the qualified withdrawal grounds and exact access to historical answers. It does not return the withdrawn answer as current, infer that there are no architectural findings, recategorize the original investigation as failed, or silently invoke a producer. A request requiring a diagram has no current answer fulfilling that requirement; a historical diagram remains accessible through exact historical access. Preserve the distinction between present fulfillment and the earlier attempt's outcome. The application chooses how to present this condition under its outcome contracts; API representation and UI wording are implementation choices. The user can explicitly request a fresh investigation, whose accepted designations can supply a new current answer without reinstating any withdrawn designation.

| Existing capability | Migration selection contract |
| --- | --- |
| `summarize` | The current designated answer of the retained summary request; exact prior captures remain accessible. |
| `explain`, `decompose`, `examine` | Preserve the exact selected prior information as subject; select the current designated answer to that investigation request. Decomposition supplies qualified smaller aspects without a contribution tree. |
| `modules` | The designated discovery method's captured population and qualification. |
| `organization` | The specified organization method's findings and scheme qualification. |
| `dependency-structure`, `dependency-children`, `dependency-parents` | The specified dependency method's findings, scope and coverage. |
| `inspect` | Its mechanical inspection plus explicit subject-associated retained information; exact-investigram inspection preserves that contribution and applicable history. No single rule replaces this mixed contract. |
| Effective export information | The export method's qualified findings and method-selected naming/support under the naming rule below, not a newly invented public Lens. |

Naming follows the same question/method boundary. A method-specific module, export or dependency answer uses the identifying information selected by that method and captured basis; an interpreted identification does not silently rename its findings or change deterministic lookup handles. For example, a mechanical module named `breaker.ts` can also have an interpreted identification “Recovery controller.” General access to current identification returns all standing qualified identifications, and inspection can expose the associated alternatives. If an association carrying the mechanical identification is withdrawn, a query requesting that method's findings can still return the original identification with applicable withdrawal context. Presentation can label or distinguish supplied alternatives, but must preserve their qualified meaning. No preferred-name or canonical-name mechanism is introduced.

For example, a summary's properly redesignated answer can be selected without rerunning its original producer. A module inventory does not replace its captured population with whichever agentic account is newest. A display selection must not silently drop a competing designated answer. Stable enumeration does not imply ordering or authority among claims; mechanical semantic determinism remains binding.

### Exact prior information as a subject

An investigation of selected content targets the exact information selected: an immutable investigram, an exact content selection or an exact designated answer spanning contributions. It does not silently resolve the originating Query result to whichever designation is now current. Preserve the originating question/result and separately available displacement context so the investigation can understand and, where appropriate, redesignate that answer.

If D1 was selected and D2 later displaces it, the subject is still D1. Choosing current information can produce another exact selection but cannot retarget an existing request. An arbitrary selected phrase need not create an Entity; the resulting investigation can explicitly identify a thing when justified. References to local graph components similarly identify an occurrence before any new Entity is introduced.

Initial delivery follows the [selected-content acquisition baseline](association-organization-and-exposure.md#acquisition-baseline-context-and-discovery), including the enclosing item for a selection within an item. The investigator can acquire other content and associations from the owning investigram when useful. No separately generated prose subject description is required.

This describes the semantics of supported contextual selections, not a requirement to implement arbitrary diagram-component selection or a third universal input type in this delivery.

This expands supported input meaning without making every Projection ID a valid subject in every interface. Typed application requests and exact reference mappings preserve subject kind/applicability. Existing CLI answer aliases continue to denote Projections for `present`; they are not silently reinterpreted as Entity or investigram handles. No follow-current operation or new CLI syntax is required here.

**Rationale and consequences.** The selected information is the user's question context. Changing its meaning underneath the request would undermine both reuse and qualified analysis. The implementation chooses reference packaging, while exact meaning and traceability remain mandatory.

### Answer identity and request reuse

Within the supported Session, otherwise identical qualified answers have the same logical Projection identity independently of lookup spelling or route. The canonical effective inputs remain: Session identity context and responsible identity-method version; resolved subjects with meaningful collection order/roles; Lens and normalized parameters; captured supporting basis and selected information; supporting methods, evidence and relevant outcomes; answer-relevant qualifications; and retained content, association/designation and standing/cause selection snapshots. Derived fields must be fixed by those inputs.

Full immutable payload consistency is mandatory. Move invocation-specific resolution/lookup context out of the Projection; preserve answer-relevant qualification in it. An engine invocation ID alone does not distinguish equivalent answers, while a meaningful change in selected support or organizing history can. Preserve method versioning, fixed references and kind-aware normalization. Matching prose is not sufficient equality. No physical memoization, global deduplication, cross-session identity or new universal View formula is required.

Lookup spelling, candidate-search coverage and reference status belong to resolution context unless they qualify the selected answer itself. Inspection of established matches under incomplete lookup does not claim exhaustive matching. Consequential displayed lookup context affects the View under existing family rules. Non-View resolution/usage results use occurrence correlation, not fabricated Projection identities.

Investigation-request equivalence uses the declared operation/question, resolved subjects with meaningful roles/order, normalized parameters, explicitly selected input context and requested result contract, including required analysis content or forms. Different required results make different requests: asking an investigator to explain a breaker is not equivalent to asking it to produce a state diagram, even if the explanation happened to include suitable state information. Matching subjects or reusable content alone does not establish request equivalence. Concrete key representation and normalization are implementation choices; no semantic matching of arbitrarily worded questions is required.

Distinguish request equivalence from reuse of supporting work. A different request can consume retained information without becoming the earlier request or automatically sharing its Query-result identity. Several consuming Lenses can still declare the same underlying investigation work; changing the consuming Lens alone does not make that work different. A request only to render an exact retained answer differently is presentation, not a new investigation, when the retained content supports it. This clarification introduces no broader cross-request answer-matching mechanism.

Ordinary requests reuse compatible completed operation outcomes within Session validity. New retained information alone does not force a new invocation/result identity or rerun; exact inputs and explicitly selected request context still matter. Automatic context acquired during execution is retained provenance, not an automatically changing request key. Reconsideration flags do not invalidate reusable work. A new Projection selection can reuse an earlier producer result. Preserve relevant partial/failed reusable outcomes and current retry/Session limits. An explicit request to investigate again is distinct from this ordinary reuse policy.

The user can explicitly request a fresh investigation of the same question, regardless of whether its earlier answers are standing, displaced, withdrawn, incomplete or marked for reconsideration. External reasons, such as a changed agent model, are sufficient motivation; no recorded change or supplied justification is a prerequisite. The request starts a new producing attempt under the currently supported producer configuration and acquires current qualified context under the applicable input contract. It must not be satisfied solely by returning the earlier completed investigation. This does not require discarding reusable supporting mechanical work, refreshing pinned program inputs or bypassing Session validity, resource limits or hosted-execution authorization.

Preserve the new invocation, actual producer/model provenance, deliveries and any accepted contributions separately from the earlier attempt. Existing Entity identities remain reusable. The fresh attempt does not automatically supersede earlier designations, reinstate withdrawn associations or clear reconsideration causes. Its qualified declarations can revise an earlier Query result or add an alternative under the normal rules. Failure or incomplete fulfillment preserves earlier information. Ordinary retrieval and exact presentation remain available without new production. The application must support the explicit fresh-investigation intent; its API spelling and UI gesture are implementation choices, and no new CLI command is required. Model selection/configuration UI is not added by this requirement.

A fresh investigation of a retained question preserves the earlier Query result as the organizing target for that question's answers. Supply that target and its question context to the producer; when the fresh attempt supplies a new or revised answer, designate it for that target through addition or explicit supersession. Separate invocation history does not isolate the new answer from the question being repeated. Subsequent ordinary retrieval follows the existing standing rules: supersession displaces exactly the named earlier designation; an addition leaves earlier standing answers available alongside the new one. Return all standing alternatives distinctly. Production recency confers no priority, and historical answers remain exactly accessible. This reuses cross-invocation result designation rather than introducing a new selection policy.

A fresh investigation can succeed without adding, superseding or withdrawing any association. After actually performing the investigation, it may return exact references to an existing answer and report that no organizing change is warranted. Preserve the new attempt's outcome, actual provenance and deliveries without requiring duplicate content or declarations merely to represent success. The requested result contract still applies: an empty response does not satisfy a diagram requirement, while an exact existing answer can satisfy it if its qualified content meets the contract. An explicit retained endorsement that earlier findings still hold is itself a new qualified assessment, subject to normal content and association rules; it neither silently strengthens the earlier findings nor clears their concerns.

This capability does not implement target-and-cause reassessment, downstream clearance or automatic investigation scheduling. A fresh answer may reach the same conclusion without clearing concerns on earlier contributions.

Retain the existing family separation, immutable payload and support rules, frozen continuation normalization, explicit arrangement inputs and authoritative usage finalization. Advance responsible method versions when semantics change. Unrelated accumulated work cannot broaden a Lens's declared population.

### Exact presentation and references

Exact retained presentation is distinct from a new Lens application. It names a retained Projection and new presentation inputs, not a new subject, information question or input binding. Resolve only fixed support. Preserve selected basis, content, associations/designations, standing/reconsideration snapshots, qualifications and relevant outcomes. Do not look up subjects, select current history, run Evaluation or acquire new evidence. Session validity checks remain separate. Current status requires fresh qualified access, not silent enrichment of an old capture.

Support existing mechanical families, investigation request/historical-inspection variants and associated-inspection composites. Preserve composite versus mechanical-support identity: presenting a composite includes its frozen associated selection; presenting its precise mechanical support is a different supported answer, not a reconstruction of the composite. Existing exports support uses the same boundary. Required missing support is an internal defect. Unknown, foreign, wrong-kind, mixed fresh/retained or malformed requests produce explicit non-View outcomes without fallback. Valid failed, unavailable, empty or partial retained answers remain valid targets.

Compatibility uses actual supplied content, qualified materialization, expansions and declared preconditions. Fresh planning uses prospective requirements; both paths use shared contracts. If requested presentation requires unselected information, refuse it with new-request guidance. Supported fallback preserves explicit choices and discloses the changed Presentation. A renderer receives resolved qualified content and supplied reference populations, never a mutable store, lookup callback or current-history selector.

Retain supported exact paging, format and disclosure behavior. Continuations resolve only against frozen listings; malformed tokens are refused, well-formed absent tokens retain the qualified unknown-continuation View and failed status, and out-of-range numeric pages can be explicitly empty. Preserve literal tokens in a disjoint identity space. Association restart clears the continuation only; revision/history paging applies answer-wide while each displayed status reports its own totals/omissions. No page acquires newly retained history. Separately label inspect-original/current-history actions as new selection.

`present` remains the CLI operation for exact answer references; fresh Lens commands reject presentation-only paging flags with guidance. Preserve explicit fresh versus retained mode, neutral retained titles, the exact subject meaning of the original investigation of selected content and current fresh-request option restrictions. A manually requested `present` applies ordinary defaults (first page/no continuation, source detail off, interface format default); the answer reference is not a previous View with inherited options. Generated continuation requests preserve other effective format/source/paging choices explicitly, including a still-unknown association token, and offer a separate restart. Structured continuation entries accompany supported Views so clients need not reconstruct commands from scalar page counts. One-shot output offers lifetime/shell guidance, not executable continuations into an expired Session.

Stable compact answer aliases remain Session-local and separate from subject bindings. Precise IDs are supported. Allocate aliases during coordination before arrangement, including worker execution; renderer output requires no lookup or allocation. Existing bindings cannot be stolen or retargeted, and aliases resolve only through their namespace, never hash-prefix search. A locally bound alias denotes its local target even if its spelling came from another Session. Unsupported/foreign targets and one-shot answer-as-subject attempts retain explicit guidance, while literal names after `--` keep literal semantics. No last-answer alias or persistent lookup service is introduced.

Retained presentation binds only fixed answer populations; newly binding previously unbound support can affect the spelling of later allocations but never existing ones. Preserve mechanical-before-associated subject-binding schedules and composite alias selection. The composite is the delivered answer target; support aliases are allocated when actually targeted, except valid retained-answer resolution targets exposed under their existing rules.

Exact presentation returns the retained Projection ID, never publishes a replacement Projection. View identity continues to include the responsible Presentation method, exact answer, effective choices and consequential lifetime/reporting context, with explicit fresh/retained mode. Preserve existing family inputs: neutral titles where used, no fabricated resolution context or current-attempt report on the retained route, association continuation/revision page for investigation arrangements, authoritative usage on finalized investigation Views, and exact mechanical-base View identity for composites. Kind-aware reference normalization keeps alias and precise-ID targeting equivalent. Preserve separate request occurrence/raw spelling records, frozen unknown-token handling and usage-independent arrangement keys. A retained presentation does not replay original provider diagnostics or attempt/reuse usage.

Current Session invalidation/shutdown and one-shot expiry still bound access. Historical content is not thereby wrong over its basis, but can be unavailable under the supported lifecycle. Keeping output/observations does not keep live references. Persistence, reopening, eviction or invalidated-Session reading require separate decisions.

**Rationale and alternatives.** Repeating a pinned request can select newer retained information; bounded JSON is not a substitute for exact retained support. These rules preserve the existing exact-presentation operation while replacing old correction-tree vocabulary with exact declarations and snapshots. They do not add a new answer archive or a UI design.

### Qualified application access

Information requests identify Lens, subject designation, semantic parameters and supported input context separately from client presentation. The application API validates runtime input; invalid requests produce qualified resolution outcomes, while internal invariant violations remain defects. Exact retained requests identify answers. Usage and unsuccessful resolution are not Projections. Separate request occurrence, current work and delivery context from immutable answer meaning.

Deliver complete resolved qualified content for the currently eager families through the intended internal API, with selected content, support/bases, relevant outcomes, metadata, relationships and full applicable association/designation/status snapshots. Do not make bounded CLI JSON the content contract or substitute a general store handle. Preserve composite/support distinctions and precise references. Further history can be exposed through supported exact access without embedding every record in every answer, provided no material qualification is hidden and bounded delivery is explicit.

The application has access to exposure, provenance, amendment and cause information with precise traceability, whether as raw engine records or faithful mappings onto application items. Investigrams need not be hidden. Application selection must not silently discard equally standing alternatives. Clients can choose which supplied answer to display while preserving the qualified meaning of that selection; method-specific findings must not appear unaffected by applicable displacement merely because their content remains selected. An exact Projection preserves its old snapshot; a fresh access can disclose later status separately.

Precise IDs suffice for answers, candidates and subjects of investigations of selected content. Existing Session aliases remain accepted by shared request coordination where supported; preserve original spelling for attribution and current refusal behavior. Compact references are CLI typing affordances, not a required external-agent/GUI design. Do not move reference lookup into rendering.

Deliver clone-safe plain structured data without compiler objects, functions, store handles or class instances. Keep host lifetime/publication context, repository root or its unavailability, and method provenance beside the answer. One-shot expiry affects presentation, not answer identity. Delivery is an internal value, not another retained Projection/View or stable external serialization promise. Exact access invokes no providers, restores no Session and charges no inference. Preserve publication, cancellation, audience classification, credentials and observation contracts independently.

### Qualified construction and retained support

Interface-independent core selects qualified subjects, relationships, standing and populations and constructs immutable answers from materialized information. Presentation arranges rows, labels, excerpts, pages, omissions and disclosure, then renders. Core does not import CLI View/layout types. For current eager families, resolve typed immutable content with complete selected records, Claim contexts, captured support and relevant outcomes; construction performs no analysis, source reads or display bounding. These are internal values, not another stored pipeline stage or CLI JSON schema. Future lazy materialization/pushdown needs its own qualified execution contract; rendering never initiates Evaluation.

Preserve each mechanical family's selected meaning: modules and exports include symbol/origin, documentation associations, composition and support; organization includes population, direct groups/placements, exceptions, embedded module answers and qualified artifact summaries; dependencies include all selected occurrences, non-edge requests, recognition coverage, module/organization expansions, opaque subjects and unestablished roots. Full supporting populations remain distinct from displayed/selected counts. Rendering preserves project-first ordering, established traversal/duplicate behavior, external collapse and bounds with explicit omissions. Beyond-bound content remains available through qualified core access.

Organization summaries remain qualified aggregations of materialized support, not independent claims about file contents. With partial placement, count only established module associations. The `unanalyzed` remainder means materialized artifacts with no supplied module/documentation association, not proof none exists; opaque-boundary counts concern that same remainder. Complete classification requires available and fully materialized relevant artifact/documentation, module and placement support with completed evaluations. Materialized detail alone is insufficient; not-requested detail differs from empty/incomplete classification. Preserve the explicit predicate in the arranged output and complete-case wording. Incomplete classification retains the notice “classification incomplete” and explains that no module or documentation association was established in the supplied information, with configured-project scope. Use conservative repository/placement qualification for valid zero-group populations and resolution qualification for unsuccessful lookup. Keep numeric calculations and epistemological meaning intact.

Investigation answers now select exact contribution/content/association/designation information, qualified inconsistencies and complete relevant standing/cause snapshots rather than root/child replacement traversals or a primary correction endpoint. Associated inspection derives from explicit subject associations, not prose/evidence mentions. Retain all declared matches, including off-page ones, with their coverage and mechanical basis. Explicit referent discovery is a separate supported lookup, not an unannounced expansion of that inspection question. Canonical enumeration is stable but does not confer claim priority.

Retain the full derived unpaged state needed for each answer at construction. Do not reconstruct it later from current history, a watermark or an assumed sufficient evidence closure. Exact records carry source bodies; snapshots carry history-dependent selections, standing, causes and meaningful relationships. Include complete proximal cause references without claiming enumeration of every possible path. Later changes create fresh selections, never new payloads under an old answer identity. Absolute unrelated Session counters alone must not change otherwise identical answer meaning.

The family outcome rules continue to distinguish valid empty/partial/unavailable mechanical answers, accepted/limited/failed/unavailable investigation selections, exact existing information and associated inspection with zero matches. Unsettled lookups and usage do not create fictitious answer artifacts. Module-only inspection routed through organization uses its embedded module answer as the associated mechanical basis; mixed/group inspection uses its actual organization basis. Selection records preserve Session/method, variant, Lens, subjects/true parameters, relevant outcomes, mechanical support and frozen semantic state. Validate types and Session scope atomically. Retained unavailable selections preserve kind/code/diagnostic; provider body/status/request IDs remain invocation reporting. Such a selection is not a reusable evaluation that suppresses an eligible retry.

Coordination owns binding. Preserve existing mechanical discovery/group/module/dependency binding schedules and all-match association binding before pagination. For the changed contribution family, bind only explicit validated supplied subject populations and exact eligible references; remove composition-parent/descendant and primary-replacement assumptions. Claims, evidence, item addresses and association addresses do not automatically acquire Entity aliases. Validate the complete binding request before allocation. Existing bindings remain append-only with stable collision behavior. Binding ports expose no selection, enumeration, retrieval, Evaluation or refresh; parent rendering/finalization cannot bind. Unsuccessful resolution binds only actual prerequisite populations and delivered candidates, never a discarded inspection answer as a side effect.

Preserve bounded presentation, continuation and omission disclosures independently of complete application content. Root/descendant traversal algorithms do not apply to the flat contribution model; choose bounds for its selected units explicitly and document them. This does not authorize silently omitting qualification or inventing a primary alternative. Existing paging/default behavior remains as stated in [exact presentation](#exact-presentation-and-references). Displayed-subset provenance and source groups reflect what the View actually exposes, not every internally resolved record. Human source disclosure is distinct from merely holding support. New exposed fields need audience/classification coverage; non-View reports must not become unclassified source-export paths.

Verification covers full qualified construction versus bounded output, relevant versus population-wide counts, partial/unavailable/unrequested classification, source classification, binding rejection without partial allocation, off-page support, collisions and direct/worker finalization. Compare fresh/accumulating/reordered semantic outputs without discarding meaningful fields. Preserve exact reconstruction across later declarations, answer/View/report identity, stable repeated finalization, alias normalization, unknown/empty pages and actual usage changes. Replace old whole-evaluation exemptions, primary replacement branches and displaced trees with exact producer timing, alternatives, association membership and per-producer exposure cases in the plan. Research capture remains selected for the research question; these checks do not reinstate universal event export.

**Rationale and consequences.** Frozen semantic support preserves exact answers while allowing history to accumulate. Whole-headed replacement carries forward mechanical qualification, reference and presentation obligations while removing tree-specific algorithms. This is required conformance in the plan, not a future optional migration or a new complete-output archive.

## Consequences and subsequent work

This decision changes engine integration and history vocabulary while preserving outer application meaning. The migration verifies every existing Lens family and reports concrete conflicts instead of silently weakening identity or method contracts. Reassessment, GUI selection/navigation and initial product catalogue remain separate. The application specification and core concepts are revised together; no new research recording or mandatory generic ancestry mechanism follows.
