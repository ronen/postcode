## Context

Mechanical analysis and agentic interpretation can both introduce things, make qualified claims about them, and organize uses of earlier information. Maintaining separate retention regimes makes cross-producer reuse, amendment and exact access unnecessarily inconsistent. A common contribution model must preserve the difference between a verified mechanical finding and an interpretive judgment about its meaning or use.

Stable reference does not establish a single correct model or semantic continuity across program changes. The supported lifecycle is the current unchanged-input Session. [Association organization](association-organization-and-exposure.md) defines uses of information; [qualified application answers](qualified-answers-over-contributions.md) remain a separate level.

## Decisions

### Identified things and inquiry results

An Entity is an explicitly identified thing in the system under study, distinct from claims about it. Modules, symbols, groups, states, instance roles and deliberately identified architectural relationships or patterns can be Entities. Identification of a relationship does not establish its occurrence, participants or behavioral conditions; those remain qualified claims. A claim or a graph edge does not become an Entity merely by being addressable.

Subject remains the role of what a Lens investigates or a claim concerns. Entities, relationships, collections, exact information selections and investigrams can play that role. Subject designation does not establish a supporting basis, a program input, or cross-account correspondence. Claim means asserted information, not proven fact; Claim context preserves method, evidence, scope, guarantee and limitations. Properties and facets retain their existing descriptive roles. Relationship claims can connect participants other than Entities, including recorded documentation and its subject.

A Query result is the organizing identity for the answer to a particular engine investigation invocation. It is separate from Entity: it concerns the inquiry, not a thing in the system under study. Recognized ordinary reuse of the same request reuses its existing result identity; a new user action does not necessarily mean a new invocation. An explicit fresh-investigation request instead starts a new producing attempt with its own invocation history. It preserves the earlier Query result as the organizing target for the repeated question's answers through explicit cross-invocation designations. Result designations select exact information. Neither the result nor its designation is a Projection.

The invocation is the particular request with its subjects, parameters, explicit context and requested result contract; its Query result is a separate identity organizing designated answers. A continuing result identity lets a later inquiry revise the answer to that question without inferring it from everything known about its Entities. Descriptions of a breaker collectively need not answer a particular question about concurrent callers.

New identifications use submission-local keys. PostCode resolves known identities and allocates new Session identities before retaining the atomic contribution. A later producer can reuse exact identities supplied in context. Reuse means referring to the previously identified thing, not endorsing its descriptions. Names are not identity keys. Independent discoveries may remain distinct until qualified correspondence is established. No automatic merge, split, alias reassignment or Entity supersession follows from a correspondence claim.

Provider-native identity is resolved under its explicit identity rule and basis scope before common-ID allocation. Map the provider/native-key/scope combination to a common identity; do not use a native key as the common ID or infer equivalence across scopes. Preserve existing mechanical identity guarantees. An interpreted Entity's continuity follows its explicit identification and qualified origin, not a new guarantee of objective uniqueness. Hypothetical and intended Entities retain their modality and provenance; matching a name does not establish implementation or acceptance.

Identification supplies a human-readable name or designation and enough qualified context to distinguish the referent. Assigned labels are distinguishable from names in the system under study. Identification can be structured mechanical information and need not include a complete behavioral definition. Several standing identifications can concern one Entity without a canonical name or description.

Exact identity resolution remains valid after identifying information is displaced. Name search uses qualified populations and preserves zero/one/many results, coverage and standing. Support historical/displaced-name lookup, routinely or by explicit request, with the historical match and access to current identifying information. Do not silently recast it as a current name or infer a rename from matching text. History defaults and user continuation are interface policy; fuzzy search is not required.

**Rationale and alternatives.** A separate interpreted-Entity registry duplicates things already known mechanically. Treating every addressable record as Entity erases the useful distinction between the system under study and PostCode's inquiry history. Explicit stable identities plus qualified descriptions provide continuity without universal semantic identity. The cost is retaining ambiguity and multiple descriptions honestly.

The narrower alternative of treating Query results as an Entity kind was considered: it would still require result-specific designation rules, so it would not remove the structural distinction. Use separate concepts with shared association machinery, without introducing a new named umbrella concept. Common internal types/storage are implementation choices; an umbrella term can be reconsidered if actual usage warrants it.

### Immutable analysis contributions

An Investigram is an immutable, addressable analysis contribution. It carries keyed Analysis content, referent information, evidence context, provenance, qualifications, organizing declarations and any qualified guidance or inconsistency metadata. Mechanical, agentic and future hybrid production are mechanisms, not an exhaustive taxonomy of claims. Structure or acceptance does not raise a claim's epistemological guarantee.

Neither all mechanical output nor every claim in a mechanically produced bundle is verified. Verification belongs to a particular method, basis, assumptions and scope; a hybrid producer label cannot substitute for attribution of its contributing methods.

A submission contains a flat pool of investigrams accepted atomically. There is no root/child composition tree. Content references and result designations identify meaningful connections and selected information. The provenance of an investigation of selected content identifies its exact subject; it neither appends children to an earlier result nor asserts program structure. Investigram organization is deferred, including mechanical, agentic and UI-driven grouping.

Each investigram's content, declarations and qualifications remain attributable to its producer and actual inputs. Independently produced contributions accepted together do not acquire one another's exposure. Shared provenance can be represented once where truthful; a producer that creates several investigrams from one input history may attribute that history to all of them. Acceptance batching alone is not production.

Every submitted content item must be reachable through an association, including a result designation. Validate reachability across the whole submission rather than demanding that each investigram associate its own items. This does not require a new public Query result for every compiler phase. An organizing contribution can associate an independently produced finding. It does not thereby own that finding or confer its method guarantee on its organizing judgment.

An organizing-only contribution names the retained inputs it actually used as its basis, not source it never read. If source is also supplied and cited, capture both source and retained inputs. The selected finding preserves its original source basis separately; reuse does not attribute that source acquisition to the organizer. A contribution containing only meaningful qualified declarations is allowed; filler prose is not required.

Partition to preserve applicable qualification and useful reuse. Separate identifying investigrams are useful when general identification would otherwise inherit a narrower behavioral scope. Group coherent identifying information where appropriate; no default separate partition or child structure is required for every introduced Entity. Each partition carries the qualifications that apply: identify its claims, retain their necessary limitations, and remove only limitations that concern excluded claims. Do not broaden applicability by editorial omission. Identifying a state category need not inherit a particular behavioral sampling configuration; identifying a scenario role may need that configuration to specify which role it denotes.

Orientation can locate related information without restating its behavioral claims. Repetition widens the surface that later declarations may need to address. Smaller units can narrow future acquisition and displacement, but do not remove their own producer's inherited exposure. A heavily exposed synthesis's identification contribution remains exposed; partitioning is not reassessment.

Investigrams have no implicit claim order or authority based on production time. Exposure, derivation and amendment give explanatory ancestry; no separate partial-order service or generic acyclicity mechanism is required. Explicit architectural ordering retains its meaning. Acceptance positions remain available for timing and reference rules without becoming last-writer precedence.

**Rationale and alternatives.** Keeping mechanical findings outside investigrams perpetuates two organizing mechanisms. Converting all information into atomic propositions or a reconciled graph imposes precision the application does not promise. A flat immutable contribution pool preserves qualified prose and structured findings without a second grouping ontology. The migration deliberately removes tree-dependent behavior instead of retaining an unneeded hierarchy.

### Production and execution

An investigation operation describes reusable work independently of the Lens consuming it. Its request identifies information need, subjects, parameters and relevant input context. The evaluation layer selects compatible retained outcomes and obtains missing work through producers. Lenses construct Projections from materialized information; construction and rendering do not invoke analysis. The engine may supply several contributions for one application answer, and a Lens may combine several operations. No one-to-one mapping is required.

Mechanical analysis may produce reusable contributions incrementally across multiple accepted submissions; atomicity applies to each submission, not necessarily the entire analysis. Evaluation outcomes remain distinct. Not every internal compiler phase needs a public Query result. These permissions do not add intermediate agentic submission or weaken its explicit readiness/stop contract.

Define an internal producer/contribution boundary for common acceptance and retention. Mechanical wrappers translate provider findings, identity scope and qualification into contributions. Agentic wrappers coordinate dialog, actual delivery tracking, submission and repair. They need not share execution protocols, credentials or compiler internals.

Within agentic execution, preserve two responsibilities: an outer domain-analysis boundary accepts the operation, subject and context, coordinates acquisition/submission validation, and returns an evaluation outcome; an inner agent-communication boundary exchanges instructions, messages, tool responses and completion/failure signals, encapsulating provider protocol, authentication and transport without defining investigation semantics. Dialog coordination connects them; the investigating agent or its test double is the communication participant. Evaluation and Session handling own retention/reuse policy. Interface names and module layout remain implementation choices. Production provenance distinguishes findings from organizing judgments even in one accepted submission. An explicit deterministic organizing policy can also author declarations under its own method. Mechanical findings do not themselves decide their organizing use; that belongs to explicit wrapper/organizing policy.

Equivalent provider recomputation reuses the established qualified contribution, including when an identifying association has been displaced or withdrawn. A cache miss or incidental provider rerun does not authorize a new association or renew withdrawn information. Reusing an old association as a result member does not reinstate it; standing remains available. Host equality decisions are not analyzer deliveries and create no exposure. Changed inputs, methods or findings can justify new information under their actual scope. Deliberate reassertion is an explicit organizing action with its own qualification, not an implicit wrapper side effect. No general prohibition of reassertion is adopted.

Each missing agentic operation uses a fresh dialog, including bounded repair, with no opaque memory carried from previous operations. It can request additional qualified information adaptively.

Initial/prefetched context and subsequently requested context obey the same capture, qualification, provenance and exposure contracts. Prefetching is an execution choice, not a distinct evidence policy. The result contract applies to the assembled submitted result, not necessarily one message. Submission readiness must be explicit. Stop before submission retains no unaccepted investigram; late submission is rejected. Submitted content enters normal whole-submission validation; repair remains within the execution guard. Communication failure does not turn unsubmitted partial dialog into accepted content. Multi-operation dialog grouping remains deferred.

Compatible outcome reuse preserves original content and provenance. A different consuming Lens or newly accumulated information alone does not require another investigation. Request equivalence preserves the requested operation/question, effective subjects, parameters, explicitly selected context and result requirements. Reusing supporting information does not by itself make different investigation requests equivalent. Runtime-acquired context is production history, not an automatically changing reuse key. Reconsideration does not invalidate a reusable outcome or automatically schedule reassessment. Explicit user-requested fresh investigation bypasses reuse of the requested completed investigation under the [fresh-investigation contract](qualified-answers-over-contributions.md#answer-identity-and-request-reuse); it does not discard reusable dependencies or automatically renew displaced associations.

**Rationale and alternatives.** A common boundary can be tested before native migration without forcing compilers into agent dialogs. Treating every recomputation as new knowledge would let routine execution silently undo organizing judgments. Conversely, suppressing a method's actual findings would confuse information with its standing. Preserve both through the [Lens answer contract](qualified-answers-over-contributions.md#lens-answers-and-engine-selection).

### Access and outcomes

The investigating agent obtains program evidence only through PostCode's subject-based interfaces and shared acquisition.

Its analysis queries go through Evaluation, which reuses retained analysis or obtains missing mechanical work. Mechanical work requested through the dialog retains its own producer attribution; receiving it is a separate qualified delivery to the agent. It can navigate qualified Entities, relationships, organization and opaque artifact/documentation references, and request captured material by reference. There is no independent filesystem discovery, path read, shell, mutation or web access. Unsupported mappings/unavailable contents return qualified responses. Repository content is data to analyze, not instructions. Full captured source can be acquired within execution limits; human-facing source restrictions do not reduce the agent's evidence surface. Coverage, chunks and omissions remain attributable.

Shared acquisition preserves captures, generated-output exclusion, Session validity and input change detection. Organization membership does not establish documentation association. An investigation of selected content is not confined to the earlier contribution's references: additional supported evidence and qualified history remain accessible. Agent delivery, hosted transmission, human output and research observations remain distinct. Preserve intentional hosted enablement, credential isolation, cancellation, execution ownership and usage attribution under their existing accepted decisions.

Separate contribution validity, request fulfillment and substantive correctness. No returned contribution can result from connectivity/authentication failure, file-read failure, execution limits or failure to submit. Malformed structure/references are not accepted; repair may be attempted. Once a contribution is valid and submitted, retain it and process all declarations normally even if required information is absent or the producer reports inability. Report the unmet contract/inability separately. Established empty information differs from missing/unavailable information. A result that meets contracts can still be substantively wrong; neither schema validation nor reported success establishes truth.

Limited coverage, uncertainty and no useful new findings do not by themselves constitute investigation failure: a submitted qualified account of those limits can be accepted. Success does not require additions, supersessions or withdrawals; a completed investigation can leave existing findings and associations unchanged. Distinguish that outcome from failure to execute or an unfulfilled result contract. Investigator-written qualifications can caveat claims but cannot raise their epistemic status; validation checks their required presence, structure and attribution, not their truth. Preserve whether a known shortfall was reported by the producer or determined by PostCode's contract checks, rather than treating the two as the same observation.

Preserve the existing agentic reusable-outcome policy: accepted contributions and terminal execution-limit or investigation failures are retained; communication/service failures and configuration unavailability are not reusable outcomes. Refusal, unrepaired truncation or invalid submission follow the actual termination cause. Unclassified provider failures remain credential-safe unclassified service failures; distinguish quota exhaustion from transient rate limiting when known. Ordinary unavailable evidence in a tool response need not terminate the dialog. Session interruption/invalidation retains no late reusable outcome, while diagnostics/usage remain reportable. Qualified mechanical work already retained survives an agentic failure under normal Session validity. Mechanical attempts preserve their existing failure/materialization distinctions; this decision does not recategorize a file error as a hosted-provider failure.

A valid partial contribution is not quarantined because its outer request failed to achieve everything requested. Fulfillment reporting and Presentation failure use existing application contracts. User objections to an apparently successful answer remain deferred; existing feedback to developers/researchers does not amend retained claims.

**Rationale and consequences.** Rejecting useful qualified content on incomplete fulfillment discards valid progress. Treating all structurally valid results as fulfilled hides limitations. The separate outcomes preserve both retention and honest reporting, without a new truth-certification gate.

### Retained domain and storage

Retain domain-shaped access to Entities, content/claims, Claim context, evidence, recorded assertions, contributions, declarations, outcomes and Projections. Storage-native rows, query objects, connections and compiler objects do not escape adapters. Graphs, trees, tables and paths are representations, not a required canonical store. Access must not conceal cost, failure, qualification or materialization. One consistent identity/information contract does not require one table or replacement of the storage engine; suitable provider-specific records can remain.

Preserve stable Session reference bindings, immutable captured support and relevant evaluation outcomes, best-effort unchanged-input validity, non-atomic/first-observed capture, and lifetime disclosure. Equivalent mechanical inputs, methods, requirements and completed evaluations retain deterministic semantic content and ordering apart from reference spelling and observation metadata. Random common IDs cannot determine semantic order. Retention, disposal, persistent caches, cross-process validity and observations remain separate concerns.

Module and symbol populations retain existing TypeScript identity and analysis rules, qualified names or honest anonymity, established overlapping facets, deterministic mnemonic handle generation and exact references. Common identification associations replace a single authoritative identifying claim outside exact history; no parallel interpreted version of each native Entity is created. Groups retain provider segment names, contextual repository-root labels and no invented intrinsic root name, generated handles or path selectors. Module/group name collisions preserve all matches with their kinds. Historical-name access extends lookup without fuzzy matching or successor inference.

Effective exports, recorded associated documentation and module composition remain module expansions. Dependency children/parents remain Lens questions. Preserve qualified direct subgroup containment and artifact/module placement separately from derived descendant membership, multiple placement, ambiguity, unplaced, unavailable and out-of-population outcomes. Organization schemes remain distinct qualified accounts; repository layout does not become canonical architecture. Cycle groupings remain presentation structures, not automatic Entities or selectable subjects; a future deliberate identification requires its own qualified contract.

Retain claim-relevant artifact manifests, kinds, exclusions, opaque boundaries and link evidence. Arbitrary unexamined files are not supporting input. Resolved external modules have opaque interiors and do not establish package/version identity; unexamined children are not an empty set. Resolved opaque, unresolved, indeterminate, platform-provided and unavailable dependency targets remain distinct. Preserve project-open failure, qualified encountered diagnostics, partial/unavailable work and unexpected defects; do not run unrelated compiler checks merely to find diagnostics.

Lens, Projection, Presentation and View retain their captured-basis meanings, including multi-projection Views. Evaluation outcomes are distinct immutable records/values, select only relevant outcomes, and are not overwritten by repeated attempts. Evidence/source disclosure and observation obligations still cover implemented results under their own audience and recording contracts. Persistence and broader modalities remain separate capabilities.

**Rationale and consequences.** The shared mechanism changes retention and lookup semantics, not the domain guarantees accumulated by earlier slices. All existing mechanical and agentic information migrates in the associated plan; a permanent representative-only adapter would leave the intended boundary incomplete. Intermediate breakage or coexistence can be explicit during implementation, but final conformance is required rather than backlogged.

## Adoption and subsequent work

Adopt with the associated plan, other contribution decisions, governing revisions and predecessor mappings. This does not authorize implementation or certify current conformance. The plan establishes conformance through controlled producers, early native/agentic integration, remaining migration and integrated review. Future organization, correspondence and lifetime work remain separate backlog candidates.
