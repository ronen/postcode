# Proposed revisions to docs/app-specification.md

These units state the proposed specification, including intended behavior, supported capabilities and capability gaps. Unlisted requirements remain unchanged.

## Navigation context — replace the paragraph beginning “For each retained View”

For each retained View, preserve what it shows, its supporting captured bases, and applicable discovery and derivation relationships: the View or subject from which it was opened, the known origin of a supplied reference, and earlier information contributing to a comparison or summary. Moving or closing a visible View does not erase those relationships from retained investigation context. This intended contract applies across human interfaces within their supported lifecycle; it does not require a separately stored View object or persistence after that lifecycle ends. Preserve origin relationships the interface establishes and disclose unavailable origin rather than guessing where a human obtained a reference. Human investigation navigation and general View-to-View lineage are gaps in the specified interface subset. Opening dependencies alongside an explanation, for example, retains the navigation origin without asserting that the two answers share a capture or that one is evidence for the other. [[Navigation-origin lineage](decisions/application-specification-boundaries.md#adoption-and-conformance)]

## Request coordination — replace the paragraph beginning “An interface can retain”

An interface can retain and use returned resolution information after execution finishes without observations or a new lookup. Precise candidate references support further requests within their supported Session lifetime, including selection of several candidates when the Lens accepts a collection. Later accumulation does not silently replace the displayed candidate selection. No independent Session lookup of the resolution result, history reopening, persistent planning store or new clarification dialog is required. The internal application API returns this information directly; keeping the returned data does not extend its references' lifetime. [[Precise references](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#precise-references)]

## Architectural accounts — replace the paragraph beginning “The current CLI remains”

The supported analysis subset covers configured TypeScript projects, module-originating prose interpretations and investigations of selected content within a transient unchanged-input Session. The internal application API is the access point for this subset, including development validation. End-user investigation, investigation of hypothetical or intended accounts, and general participant-material capture fall outside this subset. [[Supported interfaces](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#retire-client)] [[Conformance](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#adoption)]

## Responsibilities and boundaries — replace the paragraph beginning “Rendering and arrangement”

Rendering and arrangement do not initiate Evaluation or independently query accumulated state to broaden an answer. Interactions requiring additional information return through request coordination, Evaluation, and core construction. Standard expansions remain subject-kind detail declared before evaluation; another Lens question is not made a standard expansion by inline placement. The [qualified-construction boundary](decisions/qualified-answers-over-contributions.md#qualified-construction-and-retained-support) remains in force.

## Replace the complete “Application functionality and interface clients” subsection, preserving its existing anchor

### Application functionality and interface clients

This section specifies the intended application boundary. The decision records implementation conformance at adoption. [[Conformance](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#adoption)]

Application and investigation behavior is accessible through an explicit internal application API independently of the interface used to request it. Shared services coordinate qualified resolution, information requirements, Evaluation, answer construction, contribution selection, retention, exact retained-answer access, reference validity and operational outcomes. The API validates direct requests. Clients do not duplicate these operations; they own interaction, input handling, choice of supplied answers to display, Presentations and output delivery. [[Shared boundary](decisions/internal-application-api.md#shared-application-boundary)]

Requests distinguish information needs, exact retained-answer access and operational reports. Results preserve selected qualified content and supporting context independently of display bounds or format. Current eager families deliver full selected materialized content without imposing exhaustive analysis or eager materialization universally. Typed attribution and optional client invocation context remain distinct from answer meaning/identity. Client Presentation methods affect their artifacts, not application input or answer identity. Precise IDs identify retained targets within the supported Session. Holding data does not extend that lifetime or promise availability after disposal. Internal source-bearing support is not a ready-made public JSON or external-agent export. [[Qualified delivery](decisions/qualified-answers-over-contributions.md#qualified-application-access)] [[Precise references](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#precise-references)] [[Method provenance](decisions/internal-application-api.md#method-provenance)]

Clients own Presentations and declare requirements through shared coordination. Compatibility uses semantic content contracts and qualified materialization; arrangement cannot acquire missing information or broaden a retained answer. Presentations may be shared by compatible clients. A direct information request without a Presentation defaults to current subject-kind standard expansions supported by its answer family's content contract and may request a supported subset; exact retrieval never acquires missing support. Future costly expansions require reconsidering that default. Interfaces may expose different capability subsets. Shared negotiation also selects acquisition requirements; separating those responsibilities remains deferred design work. [[Requirements](decisions/internal-application-api.md#requirements-and-presentations)] [[Retained responsibilities](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#retire-client)]

Application work and client presentation/publication have distinct outcomes. A successfully produced or retrieved answer remains completed application work when later client presentation fails; the client still reports its failure. Direct application validity checks precede completion. Client-neutral pre-output/post-output validity policy does not own output or imply that receipt proves later display validity. [[Boundary and outcomes](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#retire-client)]

Provider/account services preserve credentials and resources independently of command handling. Preserve terminal Session abort, internal final accounting and separately observable cleanup/report-delivery failures under the defined direct lifecycle. Direct application clients execute investigation work in the calling process. Interruption is cooperative, and the Session lifetime is bounded by the host lifetime. Caller-supplied resource execution retains caller ownership. [[Terminal abort](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#terminal-abort)]

Limit CLI access to authentication. Development trials must render qualified explanations and state diagrams according to the applicable qualification and human-output obligations. Future human clients must establish disclosure verification for their actual output. The internal boundary permits later hosting without requiring a public library, network service, separate process or permanent schema. [[Interface boundary](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#retire-client)] [[Validation rendering](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#validation-rendering)]

## Participant material and agent exchange — replace the paragraph beginning “Agent investigation and exchange encompass”

Agent investigation and exchange encompass requesting supported Projections, consulting retained accounts, repeating requests, contributing material and suggesting further investigation. They are broader than a coding-agent handoff after human exploration. The same conceptual and qualification model applies without requiring an integrated conversation. External-agent investigation lies outside the supported internal application API. General participant-material ingestion and shared-context exchange remain future capabilities. No particular transport, conversation host or milestone is selected here. [[Interface boundary](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#retire-client)]

## Interaction capture and subjective reports — replace the final paragraph beginning “Retain the local application commitment”

Make contemporaneous subjective reports easy, with optional explanation and no obligation to report before continuing. This is a product choice, not a requirement to collect reports for every research question. Explicit inadequacy submission includes available actual displayed artifacts and exact output, makes missing context explicit, performs no reconstruction or new investigation, and reports delivery failure. Choosing no automatic recording must not silently discard a requested report. Do not infer subjective reports from automatic refusal or detail access. The specified interface subset lacks a human investigation surface for submitting these reports. Feedback capture and delivery remain product obligations. [[Feedback obligation](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#representation-limits-and-feedback)] [[Research gap](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#research-gap)]

## Replace the complete “Delivery and lifecycle” subsection, preserving its anchor

### Delivery and lifecycle

For research use, deliver resulting records to the designated destination required by [Research Observation Support](../foundation/product-design.md#research-observation-support). Ordinary non-research use that produces no research records needs no delivery. Development/test capture may use another destination, including explicitly excluded local storage. Whatever the destination, PostCode output is not independent repository evidence. A build type alone does not establish research purpose or delivery policy. [[Research capture](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#research-capture)]

The person conducting the research establishes its purpose and designated record destination through study instructions or configuration. That designation may cover multiple sessions; it requires neither separate per-session declarations, new application fields nor a mandatory research-mode workflow. A local destination can be designated for the study. Automatic capture alone establishes neither research use nor successful delivery to its destination. [[Research capture](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#research-capture)]

Make failure of a requested delivery visible without invalidating an otherwise delivered result, including a non-View report. Choosing not to record is neither delivery failure nor successful recording. Acceptance, post-acceptance cleanup, retention, migration, historical reading and producer-side caching remain distinct. Observations are evidence of use, not operational state for restoring a Session or reference bindings. [[Delivery obligations](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#research-gap)]

The specified interface subset provides no facility to capture and deliver research observations or human feedback. The backlog tracks this gap against the research delivery requirement. Research records must retain enough content or resolvable references for their research question, preserve actual displayed context and honest missing-context disclosure, and distinguish publication/acceptance from later cleanup. Operational diagnostics or product telemetry must not silently enter research evidence; an event stream does not automatically implement Workspace persistence. [[Research gap](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#research-gap)]

Operational request reporting describes the normalized request and its actual resolution/execution outcome independently of research capture or whether a View exists. Usage is subjectless reporting; unsuccessful resolution does not fabricate a program View. Retain actual outcomes, usage and consequential qualification in their operational roles. Research records of such interactions describe actual output and selected context without fabricating a view-produced event. [[Actual request reporting](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#request-reporting)]
