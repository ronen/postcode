# Proposed revisions to docs/app-specification.md

These units state the retirement target. The decision records implementation nonconformance at adoption and the plan covers reconciliation. Unlisted requirements remain unchanged.

## Navigation context — replace the paragraph beginning “For each retained View”

For each retained View, preserve what it shows, its supporting captured bases, and applicable discovery and derivation relationships: the View or subject from which it was opened, the known origin of a supplied reference, and earlier information contributing to a comparison or summary. Moving or closing a visible View does not erase those relationships from retained investigation context. This intended contract applies across human interfaces within their supported lifecycle; it does not require a separately stored View object or persistence after that lifecycle ends. Preserve origin relationships the interface establishes and disclose unavailable origin rather than guessing where a human obtained a reference. CLI retirement supplies no replacement navigation surface or general View-to-View lineage mechanism; that capability remains separately scoped. Opening dependencies alongside an explanation, for example, retains the navigation origin without asserting that the two answers share a capture or that one is evidence for the other. [[Retirement and conformance](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#adoption)]

## Request coordination — replace the paragraph beginning “An interface can retain”

An interface can retain and use returned resolution information after execution finishes without observations or a new lookup. Precise candidate references support further requests within their supported Session lifetime, including selection of several candidates when the Lens accepts a collection. Later accumulation does not silently replace the displayed candidate selection. No independent Session lookup of the resolution result, history reopening, persistent planning store or new clarification dialog is required. Direct application delivery returns this information without a CLI/worker envelope; keeping the returned data does not extend its references' lifetime. [[Precise references](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#precise-references)]

## Architectural accounts — replace the paragraph beginning “The current CLI remains”

The implemented analysis subset concerns configured TypeScript projects, module-originating prose interpretations and investigations of selected content within a transient unchanged-input Session. After CLI retirement it is reachable through the internal application API and development validation runners, with an authentication-only CLI and no replacement end-user investigation interface. It does not offer hypothetical/intended account investigation or general participant-material capture. These remain future capabilities rather than prerequisites for retaining the accurately described analysis subset. [[CLI retirement](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#retire-client)] [[Conformance](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#adoption)]

## Replace the complete “Application functionality and interface clients” subsection, preserving its existing anchor

### Application functionality and interface clients

Application and investigation behavior is accessible through an explicit internal application API independently of the interface used to request it. Shared services coordinate qualified resolution, information requirements, Evaluation, answer construction, contribution selection, retention, exact retained-answer access, reference validity and operational outcomes. The API validates direct requests. Clients do not duplicate these operations; they own interaction, input handling, choice of supplied answers to display, Presentations and output delivery. [[Shared boundary](decisions/internal-application-api.md#shared-application-boundary)]

Requests distinguish information needs, exact retained-answer access and operational reports. Results preserve selected qualified content and supporting context independently of display bounds or format. Current eager families deliver full selected materialized content without imposing exhaustive analysis or eager materialization universally. Typed attribution and optional client invocation context remain distinct from answer meaning/identity. Client Presentation methods affect their artifacts, not application input or answer identity. Precise IDs identify retained targets within the supported Session; compact CLI aliases are retired. Holding data does not extend that lifetime or promise availability after disposal. Internal source-bearing support is not a ready-made public JSON or external-agent export. [[Qualified delivery](decisions/qualified-answers-over-contributions.md#qualified-application-access)] [[Precise references](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#precise-references)] [[Method provenance](decisions/internal-application-api.md#method-provenance)]

Clients own Presentations and declare requirements through shared coordination. Compatibility uses semantic content contracts and qualified materialization; arrangement cannot acquire missing information or broaden a retained answer. Presentations may be shared by compatible clients. A direct information request without a Presentation defaults to current subject-kind standard expansions supported by its answer family's content contract and may request a supported subset; exact retrieval never acquires missing support. Future costly expansions require reconsidering that default. Interfaces may expose different capability subsets. Shared negotiation remains after CLI retirement, with its acquisition coupling documented for later design. [[Requirements](decisions/internal-application-api.md#requirements-and-presentations)] [[Retained responsibilities](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#retire-client)]

Application work and client presentation/publication have distinct outcomes. A successfully produced or retrieved answer remains completed application work when later client presentation fails; the client still reports its failure. Direct application validity checks precede completion. A shared publication guard expresses pre-output/post-output validity policy without owning output or implying that receipt proves later display validity. The private CLI deferral and binding scope are retired. [[Boundary and outcomes](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#retire-client)]

Provider/account services preserve credentials and resources independently of command handling. Preserve terminal Session abort, internal final accounting and separately observable cleanup/report-delivery failures under the defined direct lifecycle. Execution is in-process and cooperative; no hard preemption of synchronous compiler work or survival after host destruction is promised. Caller-supplied resource execution retains caller ownership. [[Terminal abort](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#terminal-abort)]

The investigation CLI, its worker and mechanical-family human Presentations retire. Authentication retains a small CLI entry point. Useful qualified explanation and state-diagram rendering moves to development trial support, subject to the applicable qualification and human-output obligations. Future human clients must establish disclosure verification for their actual output. The internal boundary permits later hosting without requiring a public library, network service, separate process or permanent schema. [[Client retirement](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#retire-client)] [[Validation rendering](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#validation-rendering)]

Removing the command-shaped feedback and observation implementation leaves the research/feedback capability gap described below; it does not withdraw those product obligations. The [retirement plan](plans/cli-shell-retirement.md) reconciles current implementation with this boundary. The [decision's conformance statement](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#adoption) identifies the transition and the separately backlogged gap.

## Participant material and agent exchange — replace the paragraph beginning “Agent investigation and exchange encompass”

Agent investigation and exchange encompass requesting supported Projections, consulting retained accounts, repeating requests, contributing material and suggesting further investigation. They are broader than a coding-agent handoff after human exploration. The same conceptual and qualification model applies without requiring an integrated conversation. CLI retirement removes the existing investigation-command route; the remaining internal application API is not a supported external-agent interface. General participant-material ingestion and shared-context exchange remain future capabilities. No particular transport, conversation host or milestone is selected here. [[Client retirement](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#retire-client)]

## Interaction capture and subjective reports — replace the final paragraph beginning “Retain the local application commitment”

Retain the application commitment to make contemporaneous subjective reports easy, with optional explanation and no obligation to report before continuing. This is a product choice, not a requirement to collect reports for every research question. Explicit inadequacy submission includes available actual displayed artifacts and exact output, makes missing context explicit, performs no reconstruction or new investigation, and reports delivery failure. Choosing no automatic recording must not silently discard a requested report. Do not infer subjective reports from automatic refusal or detail access. The command-shaped implementation retires without replacement: while there is no human investigation interface, feedback and research interaction capture/delivery remain an explicit capability gap, tracked for reconciliation in the backlog. No GUI feedback design is selected by retirement. [[Feedback obligation](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#representation-limits-and-feedback)] [[Research gap](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#research-gap)]

## Replace the complete “Delivery and lifecycle” subsection, preserving its anchor

### Delivery and lifecycle

For research use, deliver resulting records to the designated destination required by [Research Observation Support](../foundation/product-design.md#research-observation-support). Ordinary non-research use that produces no research records needs no delivery. Development/test capture may use another destination, including explicitly excluded local storage. Whatever the destination, PostCode output is not independent repository evidence. A build type alone does not establish research purpose or delivery policy. [[Research capture](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#research-capture)]

The person conducting the research establishes its purpose and designated record destination through study instructions or configuration. That designation may cover multiple sessions; it requires neither separate per-session declarations, new application fields nor a mandatory research-mode workflow. A local destination can be designated for the study. Automatic capture alone establishes neither research use nor successful delivery to its destination. [[Research capture](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#research-capture)]

Make failure of a requested delivery visible without invalidating an otherwise delivered result, including a non-View report. Choosing not to record is neither delivery failure nor successful recording. Acceptance, post-acceptance cleanup, retention, migration, historical reading and producer-side caching remain distinct. Observations are evidence of use, not operational state for restoring a Session or reference bindings. [[Delivery obligations](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#research-gap)]

The command-batch protocol and development filesystem sink retire with their CLI consumers. No replacement schema or delivery implementation is selected. The research/feedback capability gap is documented and backlogged while the foundation obligations remain in force. A future design must retain enough content or resolvable references for its research question, preserve actual displayed context and honest missing-context disclosure, and distinguish publication/acceptance from later cleanup. Operational diagnostics or product telemetry must not silently enter research evidence; an event stream does not automatically implement Workspace persistence. [[Research gap](decisions/cli-shell-retirement.md#research-gap)]

Operational request reporting describes the normalized request and its actual resolution/execution outcome independently of research capture or whether a View exists. Usage is subjectless reporting; unsuccessful resolution does not fabricate a program View. Retain actual outcomes, usage and consequential qualification in their operational roles. Research records of such interactions describe actual output and selected context without fabricating a view-produced event. [[Actual request reporting](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#request-reporting)]
