# Proposed revisions to docs/architectural-constraints.md

Add the following constraint groups after `Session references and retained
information` and before `Generated-output evidence boundary`. Existing groups
are unchanged.

## Retained interpretation

- Bind investigram references to immutable artifacts within their session. Requests naming a precise investigram reference target that exact artifact, without automatic redirection to a replacement. Lookup handles expose missing or ambiguous matches rather than silently preferring a replacement. Display selection of explicit replacements must use their own references; superseded originals remain inspectable. [[Investigrams](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#represent-retained-interpretation-as-investigrams)] [[Corrections](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#record-explicit-corrections-without-rewriting-earlier-interpretation)]
- Keep a retained investigram composition tree fixed. A subsequent investigation produces a separate result linked to its selected subject through provenance, not additional composition children. Keep both relationships distinct from claims about program structure or logical dependence. [[Composition and investigation provenance](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#distinguish-fixed-composition-from-investigation-provenance)]
- Carry corrections in investigram content with explicit targets, replacement investigrams, reasons, and evidence context. Construct each replacement through its correction with a composition tree disjoint from the reporting tree. Correction targets must predate acceptance of the result, including recursively accompanying corrections. Accept the operation result and its accompanying corrections as a unit. Preserve originals, composition trees, and historical projections. Retain conflicting corrections and expose conflicts and alternatives. When selecting one account, default to the endpoint produced by the most recently accepted correction across all reachable correction branches, without treating recency as credibility. Redisplay follows replacement chains and uses the selected replacement's own composition; display substitution creates no composition relationship. Do not silently correct subparts or change the subject references of subsequent investigations to point to a replacement. Reference validation does not establish semantic correctness or justify automatic propagation. [[Corrections](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#record-explicit-corrections-without-rewriting-earlier-interpretation)]

- Construct investigram citation indexes from actual context delivery, not investigator-reported relevance. Any substantive content creates a citation; correction eligibility requires complete retained prose, referent information, and qualifications delivered during the current evaluation. Keep correction targets in citation indexes. Exempt every investigram produced by an evaluation from each correction cause it produced or whose complete context it received. Apply exemption at the investigram regardless of citation path; preserve other causes and propagation through non-exempt citations. Delivery records historical exposure, not continued internal retention or comprehension. Derive and disclose reconsideration status through citations without mutating artifacts, asserting semantic dependence, or automatically regenerating results. [[Citation exposure and reconsideration](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#record-citation-exposure-and-derive-reconsideration-status)]

## Investigator access

- When supplying a corrected investigram to the investigator, deliver the exact requested artifact with an explicit correction notice, replacement accounts, correction reasons and qualifications, and applicable correction chains and conflicting alternatives. Apply this to initial context and subsequent retrieval. Record actual delivery and completeness per correction; disclose bounded omissions rather than treating a notice or reference alone as complete correction context. [[Correction-aware context delivery](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#record-citation-exposure-and-derive-reconsideration-status)]
- Provide program evidence to the investigator only through PostCode's subject-based interface and shared evidence acquisition. The subject population is the complete program-access surface; the investigator has no independent filesystem discovery, path-based reads, shell execution, mutation, or web access. [[Investigator evidence access](decisions/investigator-execution-and-evidence-access.md#integrate-interpretation-with-evaluation-and-qualified-evidence-access)]
- Treat repository content supplied to the investigator as evidence to analyze, not instructions to obey. Keep credentials outside investigator context, investigrams, and observations. [[Investigator evidence access](decisions/investigator-execution-and-evidence-access.md#integrate-interpretation-with-evaluation-and-qualified-evidence-access)]
