# Proposed revisions to docs/architectural-constraints.md

## Add “Lens applications and request outcomes” before “Views and analysis boundary”

## Lens applications and request outcomes

- Represent Lens meaning, subject/parameter applicability and cardinality, normalized parameter meaning, requirements and prospective result contracts explicitly. Keep designation, resolved subjects, program inputs/captured support and presentation/execution choices distinct. One Lens can use subject-specific implementations; shared authoring does not merge Lens and reusable operation identity. [[Lens meaning](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#represent-lens-meaning-independently-of-lookup-and-implementation)]
- Request coordination owns qualified subject lookup. Narrow name lookup by a selected Lens's applicability; resolve precise references to actual targets before checking applicability. Preserve unknown, incompatible and unmaterialized targets as different cases. Undeclared or invalid Lens parameters at the public request boundary return a resolution result. Internal violations after validation are defects, not the ordinary user-request refusal path. Share applicability definitions or validation logic across those boundaries. [[Resolution](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#coordinate-qualified-subject-resolution-outside-lens-implementations)]
- Return a self-contained resolution result for an unsettled application, including candidates, reasons, consequential criteria, qualifications and prerequisite attribution. Do not manufacture a Projection for that unsuccessful application. Preserve valid empty/partial answers, valid-but-failed/unavailable selections and independently produced prerequisite results. Returned information remains usable without observations or fresh lookup; it does not require independent Session addressability or extend candidate-reference lifetime. [[Resolution lifetime and scoped retention change](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#return-unsettled-applications-as-resolution-results)]
- Give otherwise identical qualified answers repeatable Projection identity within the supported Session independently of designation. Use the decision’s canonical effective-input set and separate invocation-specific resolution information from immutable answer payloads while preserving all effective answer inputs, full-payload consistency, method versions and frozen interpretation/revision selections. Physical memoization and cross-session identity are not required. [[Answer identity](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#give-equivalent-qualified-answers-repeatable-identity)]
- Carry actual normalized request and outcome information through publication rather than infer it from a View. Usage reports and resolution results do not manufacture a program Lens, Projection or View. Preserve generating provenance on reuse and attribute current consumption separately. [[Actual reporting](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#report-actual-requests-and-outcomes)]

## Add to “Views and analysis boundary” after its last bullet

- Use explicit qualified result/Presentation compatibility in planning for program View construction, including future Lens and Presentation additions. Declare standard expansions before Evaluation, preserve actual choices and criteria, and check materialized content against the contract before arrangement. Data-shape similarity alone does not establish semantic compatibility. A missing compatible Presentation or unmet materialization-dependent precondition without a permitted fallback returns a resolution result identifying that reason and any retained answer references; required further analysis returns through coordination. Contract associations are available at runtime. Declared fallbacks preserve explicit choices and expose consequential changes; adapters preserve meaning and qualification without hidden analysis. Resolution context supplied for displayed lookup messages does not grant arrangement lookup or Evaluation access. [[Result/Presentation contracts](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#match-qualified-results-to-presentations-explicitly)]

## Add to “Observation lifecycle and delivery” after its last bullet

- The delivery-failure rule also applies to non-View resolution results and usage reports: expose observation-delivery failure without invalidating an otherwise delivered result. Record actual output, usage and outcome without a fabricated View or view-produced event. [[Request reporting](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#report-actual-requests-and-outcomes)]
- During early development, affected structured outputs can change directly under the temporary experimental-output policy, with specific accepted preservation clauses reconciled explicitly. Preserve semantic qualification and method provenance; experimental output schema strings do not establish supported compatibility versions. Observation batches remain explicitly versioned and experimental without a historical migration promise. [[Experimental outputs](decisions/experimental-structured-outputs.md)]
