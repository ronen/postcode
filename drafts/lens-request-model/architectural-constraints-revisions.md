# Proposed revisions to docs/architectural-constraints.md

## Add “Lens applications and request outcomes” before “Views and analysis boundary”

## Lens applications and request outcomes

- Represent Lens meaning, subject/parameter applicability and cardinality, normalized parameter meaning, requirements and prospective result contracts explicitly. Keep designation, resolved subjects, program inputs/captured support and presentation/execution choices distinct. One Lens can use subject-specific implementations; shared authoring does not merge Lens and reusable operation identity. [[Lens meaning](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#represent-lens-meaning-independently-of-lookup-and-implementation)]
- Request coordination owns qualified subject lookup. Narrow name lookup by a selected Lens's applicability; resolve precise references to actual targets before checking applicability. Preserve unknown, incompatible and unmaterialized targets as different cases. Internal Lens input violations are defects, not the ordinary user-request refusal path. [[Resolution](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#coordinate-qualified-subject-resolution-outside-lens-implementations)]
- Return self-contained resolution information for an unsettled application, including candidates, reasons, consequential criteria, qualifications and prerequisite attribution. Do not manufacture a Projection for that unsuccessful application. Preserve valid empty/partial answers, valid-but-failed/unavailable selections and independently produced prerequisite results. Returned information remains usable without observations or fresh lookup; it does not require independent Session addressability or extend candidate-reference lifetime. [[Resolution lifetime and scoped retention change](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#return-unsettled-applications-as-resolution-results)]
- Give otherwise identical qualified answers repeatable Projection identity within the supported Session independently of designation. Separate invocation-specific resolution data from immutable answer payloads while preserving all effective answer inputs, full-payload consistency, method versions and frozen interpretation/revision selections. Physical memoization and cross-session identity are not required. [[Answer identity](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#give-equivalent-qualified-answers-repeatable-identity)]
- Carry actual normalized request and outcome information through publication rather than infer it from a View. Usage and resolution reports do not manufacture a program Lens, Projection or View. Preserve generating provenance on reuse and attribute current consumption separately. [[Actual reporting](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#report-actual-requests-and-outcomes)]

## Add to “Views and analysis boundary” after its last bullet

- Use explicit qualified result/Presentation compatibility in planning for every current program View path. Declare standard expansions before Evaluation, preserve actual choices and criteria, and check materialized content against the contract before arrangement. Data-shape similarity alone does not establish semantic compatibility. A missing compatible Presentation is a View-planning outcome; required further analysis returns through coordination. Resolution context supplied for displayed lookup messages does not grant arrangement lookup or Evaluation access. [[Result/Presentation contracts](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#match-qualified-results-to-presentations-explicitly)]

## Add to “Observation lifecycle and delivery” after its last bullet

- The delivery-failure rule also applies to non-View resolution and usage reports: expose observation-delivery failure without invalidating an otherwise delivered result. Record actual output, usage and outcome without a fabricated View or view-produced event. [[Request reporting](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#report-actual-requests-and-outcomes)]
- During early development, affected structured outputs can change directly under the temporary experimental-output policy, with specific accepted preservation clauses reconciled explicitly. Preserve semantic qualification and method provenance; experimental schema labels do not establish supported compatibility versions. [[Experimental outputs](decisions/experimental-structured-outputs.md)]
