# Architectural Constraints

This document states PostCode's current binding, cross-cutting architectural constraints. It is the concise operational source for rules that implementations must preserve across plans and slices. Accepted [decision records](decisions/) preserve why these constraints were adopted or changed.

The constraints conform to the adopted [product design](../foundation/product-design.md), state binding cross-cutting obligations consistent with the [application specification](app-specification.md), and use the governing terminology in [core concepts](core-concepts.md). They do not describe the implementation, repeat routine implementation conventions, or collect every consequence of every accepted decision. Descriptive implementation architecture belongs under [`docs/architecture/`](architecture/), and repeatable mechanical practices belong in [implementation conventions](implementation-conventions.md).

Substantive changes require explicit human agreement and a corresponding accepted decision record, with both updated in the same commit. Append to every constraint a short bracketed link to the decision that established or most recently changed it. Follow the [development workflow](../dev/workflow.md#documentation) when changing this document. If this document and an accepted decision disagree, treat the inconsistency as an unexpected finding rather than silently choosing or reconciling them.

## Claims and qualification

- Preserve each claim's evidence or source references, provenance and method, scope, epistemological guarantee, and limitations throughout evaluation, projection construction, transformation, storage, comparison, caching, and presentation. Shared context must remain attributable, and narrower qualification must not be erased. [[Preserve claim qualification](decisions/adopt-qualification-and-evaluation-constraints.md#preserve-claim-qualification-throughout-processing-and-presentation)]
- Derive a claim's epistemological status from its evidence and method. Confidence expressed by a person, heuristic, or generative model does not strengthen the claim. [[Preserve claim qualification](decisions/adopt-qualification-and-evaluation-constraints.md#preserve-claim-qualification-throughout-processing-and-presentation)]
- Do not strengthen or broaden a claim when transforming, aggregating, caching, comparing, or rendering it. [[Preserve claim qualification](decisions/adopt-qualification-and-evaluation-constraints.md#preserve-claim-qualification-throughout-processing-and-presentation)]
- Keep mechanically derived facts, recorded assertions, observations, and interpretations distinguishable in domain representations and human-facing views. [[Preserve claim qualification](decisions/adopt-qualification-and-evaluation-constraints.md#preserve-claim-qualification-throughout-processing-and-presentation)]
- Keep consequential qualification visible in the human-facing view. A weaker, partial, historical, asserted, observed, or inferred claim must not masquerade as a stronger one. [[Preserve claim qualification](decisions/adopt-qualification-and-evaluation-constraints.md#preserve-claim-qualification-throughout-processing-and-presentation)]
- Prefer explicit unavailability, refusal, partiality, or limitation to plausible unsupported output. [[Preserve claim qualification](decisions/adopt-qualification-and-evaluation-constraints.md#preserve-claim-qualification-throughout-processing-and-presentation)]

<a id="architectural-accounts-and-captured-bases"></a>
## Architectural accounts and captured bases

- Distinguish implemented, hypothetical and intended architecture from provenance, method and epistemological status. Recording a proposal establishes neither feasibility, acceptance nor realization. Observed behavior remains execution-scoped evidence. Preserve provenance and transition history when a proposal becomes an intention; sharing an account does not establish participant agreement or PostCode authority. [[Architectural modalities](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#architectural-modalities)]
- Apply a Lens only where its method supports the subject and modality. New architectural claims, including correspondence, conformance, contradiction and synthesized explanation, require core analysis and a qualified result. Request interpretation and presentation cannot independently derive those claims. This extends the existing program-claim boundary, including its permissions for arranging and counting supplied qualified populations, without adding evidence access. [[Architectural scope](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#results-over-captured-bases)]
- Views and Projections do not directly modify the program or silently rewrite their underlying material. Participant contributions, where supported, remain distinguishable from earlier captures and from accepted intentions. Receiving material is not itself a change to the investigated program. [[Read-only investigation](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#specialized-product-role)]
- Retain earlier Projections, supplied prior interpretation or participant material as identifiable, qualified contributions when used in a captured basis. Preserve the distinction between content supplied to an investigation, evidence claimed for a result and accounts/revisions selected into an answer; do not treat exposure as proof of reliance or endorsement. Preserve supporting associations, scope, provenance, limitations and meaningful roles or order. Do not turn retained interpretation into a captured program state or independent repository corroboration. Preserve the generated-output evidence boundary. [[Captured bases](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#inputs-and-captured-bases)]
- Preserve the meaning and provenance of entities introduced by hypothetical or intended accounts; correspondence to other accounts or implemented entities requires support and qualification. Addressability and matching labels alone establish no such correspondence. Existing implemented-state identity and Session rules remain binding. [[Architectural subjects](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#architectural-subjects)]

<a id="evaluation-and-failure"></a>
## Evaluation and failure

- Keep reusable investigation operations distinct from their consuming lenses. The evaluation layer selects retained outcomes and missing operations and coordinates execution through the domain interpretation boundary; projections consume materialized results. [[Investigation operations and lenses](decisions/investigation-operations-and-lenses.md)]
- Keep analysis applicability and availability, execution state, result materialization, and epistemological status distinct. Additional work or successful execution does not inherently make a produced claim truer or more exact. [[Evaluation and failure distinctions](decisions/adopt-qualification-and-evaluation-constraints.md#keep-evaluation-materialization-and-failure-distinctions-explicit)]
- Preserve usable qualified information from partial, stopped, or failed evaluation when the analysis method permits it. Do not make an absent result indistinguishable from an established empty result. [[Evaluation and failure distinctions](decisions/adopt-qualification-and-evaluation-constraints.md#keep-evaluation-materialization-and-failure-distinctions-explicit)]
- Keep expected operational failures, unexpected defects or broken invariants, successful results containing diagnostics or limitations, and evaluation failures that prevent the intended result distinct. Do not convert an unexpected defect into an ordinary analysis outcome merely to continue execution. [[Evaluation and failure distinctions](decisions/adopt-qualification-and-evaluation-constraints.md#keep-evaluation-materialization-and-failure-distinctions-explicit)]
- Preserve meaningful domain distinctions until presentation. Presentation may format or progressively disclose information, but must not collapse distinctions that affect what the result means or what may safely be concluded from it. [[Evaluation and failure distinctions](decisions/adopt-qualification-and-evaluation-constraints.md#keep-evaluation-materialization-and-failure-distinctions-explicit)]

<a id="investigation-initiation-and-request-attribution"></a>
## Investigation initiation and request attribution

- Obtain additional qualified information needed for request resolution through analysis, Evaluation, and core construction. Request interpretation must not independently derive program claims or the population used to resolve subjects. Preserve established candidates and consequential incompleteness, ambiguity, unavailability, or inability to resolve. [[Investigation initiation](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#investigation-attribution)]
- Distinguish the initiator of additional investigation from the actor authorizing its parent. Attribute request-scoped system-initiated work to an explicit parent and govern it by that request's purpose, constraints, and resource choices. [[Investigation initiation](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#investigation-attribution)]
- Treat work outside the parent's purpose or continuing after satisfaction or cancellation as autonomous. Autonomous work requires human-authorized resource policies; defer work exceeding those policies or materially delaying an explicit request unless authorized. Hosted-service enablement alone is not autonomous resource authorization. [[Investigation initiation](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#investigation-attribution)]
- When additional investigation influences selection, ambiguity, inability to resolve, suggestions, Views or reports, keep its trigger, parent where applicable, captured basis, relevant results and qualifications inspectable from that outcome. Preserve attribution in any research records that capture the work. Research selection does not weaken investigation attribution. Reuse does not establish freshness merely because the input has a following binding. [[Investigation attribution](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#investigation-attribution)]

## Lens applications and request outcomes

- Represent Lens meaning, subject/parameter applicability and cardinality, normalized parameter meaning, requirements and prospective result contracts explicitly. Keep designation, resolved subjects, program inputs/captured support and presentation/execution choices distinct. One Lens can use subject-specific implementations; shared authoring does not merge Lens and reusable operation identity. [[Lens meaning](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#represent-lens-meaning-independently-of-lookup-and-implementation)] [[Current request-model conformance gaps](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#adoption-and-conformance)]
- Request coordination owns qualified subject lookup. Narrow name lookup by a selected Lens's applicability; resolve precise references to actual targets before checking applicability. Preserve unknown, incompatible and unmaterialized targets as different cases. Undeclared or invalid Lens parameters at the public request boundary return a resolution result. Internal violations after validation are defects, not the ordinary user-request refusal path. Share applicability definitions or validation logic across those boundaries. [[Resolution](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#coordinate-qualified-subject-resolution-outside-lens-implementations)] [[Current request-model conformance gaps](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#adoption-and-conformance)]
- Return a self-contained resolution result for an unsettled application, including candidates, reasons, consequential criteria, qualifications and prerequisite attribution. Do not manufacture a Projection for that unsuccessful application. Preserve valid empty/partial answers, valid-but-failed/unavailable selections and independently produced prerequisite results. Returned information remains usable without observations or fresh lookup; it does not require independent Session addressability or extend candidate-reference lifetime. [[Resolution lifetime and scoped retention change](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#return-unsettled-applications-as-resolution-results)] [[Current request-model conformance gaps](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#adoption-and-conformance)]
- Give otherwise identical qualified answers repeatable Projection identity within the supported Session independently of designation. Use the decision’s canonical effective-input set and separate invocation-specific resolution information from immutable answer payloads while preserving all effective answer inputs, full-payload consistency, method versions and frozen interpretation/revision selections. Physical memoization and cross-session identity are not required. [[Answer identity](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#give-equivalent-qualified-answers-repeatable-identity)] [[Current request-model conformance gaps](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#adoption-and-conformance)]
- Carry actual normalized request and outcome information through publication rather than infer it from a View. Usage reports and resolution results do not manufacture a program Lens, Projection or View. Preserve generating provenance on reuse and attribute current consumption separately. [[Actual reporting](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#request-reporting)] [[Current request-model conformance gaps](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#adoption-and-conformance)]

## Views and analysis boundary

- Keep analysis, Evaluation and Projection construction independent of interfaces. GUI and other interface code may select Lens requests and supplied Projections, request Evaluation, implement Presentations, manage interaction and instantiate Views over one or more independently qualified Projections. They must not establish new Claims about investigated subjects or retained interpretations. New program information requires core analysis and a qualified Projection. [[Coordinated interface boundary](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#coordinate-supplied-information-without-deriving-new-program-claims)]
- A Presentation may sort, group by supplied fields, filter, progressively disclose and report exact counts over explicitly identified qualified populations supplied by its Projections. Computing an exact count of that population is explicitly allowed even if the input does not store the numeral. Beyond these supplied-population descriptions, deriving an aggregate that makes a new program claim requires core analysis and a qualified Projection. It may describe the full supplied population or a filtered/displayed subset: “87 dependencies; showing 20” is valid when 87 is established for that supplied population. Identify the population actually counted, retain each contribution's qualification and materialization, and disclose consequential aggregation, filtering and omission. A displayed count does not establish complete analysis; unsupported totals, inferred classification or correspondence, and aggregates establishing new program claims require core analysis and a qualified Projection. [[Population descriptions](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#coordinate-supplied-information-without-deriving-new-program-claims)]

- Whenever PostCode selects Projections or a Presentation, keep the choices actually made, their criteria at the time and consequential omissions inspectable. Do not reconstruct their explanation from subsequently changed defaults. This does not require a general planner or separately stored plan artifact. Do not let omitted aspects imply absence, unavailability or unimportance without support. [[Inspectable selection](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#preserve-inspectable-selection-and-parameter-meaning)]
- Classify output-form or length choices as presentation choices when they change only rendering or visible abbreviation. If satisfying them changes requested or synthesized information, express that through Projection selection or Lens parameters. [[Parameter meaning](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#preserve-inspectable-selection-and-parameter-meaning)]

- Preserve each Projection's identity, subject, Lens, parameters, captured basis, qualification and relevant outcomes when coordinating it with others. Align or overlay only through correspondence already established by the inputs or an exact identity guarantee they supply. Displaying results together does not establish changes, correlations, conformance, contradictions or flows. [[Coordinated interface boundary](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#coordinate-supplied-information-without-deriving-new-program-claims)] [[Captured bases](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#results-over-captured-bases)]
- Make additions and removals of underlying Projections visible as View composition changes. When replacing displayed information with a result based on newly captured material, expose the change of basis. Preserve the lineage needed by the investigation; research capture follows its selected question. Do not treat these as rendering-context changes or infer architectural relationships from presentation coordination or investigation lineage. [[View composition](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#results-over-captured-bases)]
- Preserve the selected answer and its qualifications during presentation-only actions, including paging and reformatting. If an interaction instead refreshes the answer, make that behavior apparent and disclose consequential changes in the answer, selection or qualifications. Disclosed reselection does not provide the same continuity as presenting an exact retained result. Identifier changes alone need not trigger warnings, and independently requested fresh investigations need not each carry comparison notices. [[Presentation continuity](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#preserve-the-selected-answer-during-presentation-changes)] [[Current CLI limitation and deferred remediation](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#adoption-and-conformance)]
- For the current eagerly retained Projection families, a supported exact-presentation request names a retained answer and arranges its fixed qualified content without subject reselection, Evaluation or acquisition of additional evidence. Preserve its selected interpretation, correction/association state and relevant evaluation outcomes as well as its captured program basis. An incompatible target or need for information outside that answer produces an explicit refusal, never silent fresh selection. Report current presentation separately from the retained answer's analysis outcome; references remain subject to the supported Session lifetime. Session invalidation does not itself invalidate a historical answer over its captured basis; the current lifecycle can make that answer unavailable. [[Exact retained presentation](decisions/exact-retained-answer-presentation.md#request-an-exact-retained-answer)] [[Outcome reporting](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#retained-presentation)]

- Do not treat the presentational permissions as an exhaustive whitelist of UI computation. Ordinary layout and interaction remain presentational when they preserve supplied meaning and qualification; new program claims remain core responsibility. [[Interface boundary](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#coordinate-supplied-information-without-deriving-new-program-claims)]

- For module, organization, dependency and investigation Projection families, including subject-associated investigram inspection, arrangement in every current or future Presentation, including the GUI, consumes the qualified information selected for its Projection. It must not independently query accumulated session state to broaden or refresh that information. New selection or refresh returns through core construction. [[Qualified construction and arrangement](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#qualified-construction)]
- This arrangement boundary permits projection-scoped lazy materialization and presentation pushdown as execution strategies planned and executed through Evaluation and core construction, preserving Lens meaning, population, qualification, evaluation materialization and retained-result semantics. Arrangement and rendering do not initiate Evaluation; an interaction needing more information issues a new request through Evaluation and core construction. Eager resolution and separately stored processing stages are not required by this boundary. [[Qualified construction and arrangement](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#qualified-construction)]
- For these Projection families, arrangement may use a narrowly scoped, session-owned reference-binding capability to allocate or return compact spellings for explicit record IDs and kinds supplied by the core for its Projection, including navigable support. Validate requests against that supplied reference population before allocation and preserve established append-only bindings. The capability returns only bindings; it must not expose selector lookup, record retrieval, session enumeration, evaluation or content refresh. Reference binding must not broaden the selected information. [[Qualified construction and arrangement](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#qualified-construction)]

- Use explicit qualified result/Presentation compatibility in planning for program View construction, including future Lens and Presentation additions. Declare standard expansions before Evaluation, preserve actual choices and criteria, and check materialized content against the contract before arrangement. Data-shape similarity alone does not establish semantic compatibility. A missing compatible Presentation or unmet materialization-dependent precondition without a permitted fallback returns a resolution result identifying that reason and any retained answer references; required further analysis returns through coordination. Contract associations are available at runtime. Declared fallbacks preserve explicit choices and expose consequential changes; adapters preserve meaning and qualification without hidden analysis. Resolution context supplied for displayed lookup messages does not grant arrangement lookup or Evaluation access. [[Result/Presentation contracts](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#match-qualified-results-to-presentations-explicitly)] [[Current request-model conformance gaps](decisions/lens-application-and-request-outcomes.md#adoption-and-conformance)]

## Program states, bindings and summary navigation

- Preserve the association of every Projection's claims and evidence with their captured basis, including meaningful contribution roles or order, capture timing where applicable and relevant limitations. Program states retain their existing guarantees. Results and coordinated Views must not imply synchronized snapshots, complete capture, equivalent evidentiary bases or cross-account correspondence unless their basis supports those claims. [[Bindings and refresh](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#preserve-immutable-results-under-bindings-and-refresh)] [[Broader basis attribution](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#results-over-captured-bases)]
- Separate designated inputs and binding policies from captured program states and retained results. For revision-oriented inputs, following resolves a changing input again on subsequent requests; pinned reuses the same captured program state. Distinguish different captures and keep new information associated with its new basis without mutating earlier results. Other inputs use source-appropriate bindings and retain the observations captured as their basis. [[Bindings and refresh](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#preserve-immutable-results-under-bindings-and-refresh)]
- Distinguish captured program-state support, interpretation supplied to a generating investigation, and the exact retained accounts and correction/association snapshots selected into an answer. The latter two contribute to the broader captured basis in their respective roles; preserve those roles and the supplied or selected content and qualifications without treating exposure as reliance. Interpretation history does not become a program state or acquire following/pinned policies. [[Presentation continuity](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#preserve-the-selected-answer-during-presentation-changes)] [[Retained contributions](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#inputs-and-captured-bases)]
- When exposing references in coding-agent context, distinguish an exact Projection over its captured basis from a request repeatable using currently bound inputs. Preserve the associations among designated inputs, binding policies, captured states and results, including a visible difference between a shown basis and what a subsequent request would select. [[Bindings and refresh](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#preserve-immutable-results-under-bindings-and-refresh)]
- Apply the exact-Projection versus repeated-request distinction to context supplied to any external design or implementation agent, including an agent conducting its own investigation. Preserve reference scope and lifetime, contributing bases and qualifications without requiring a preceding human investigation or embedded conversation. [[External participants](decisions/specialized-architectural-investigation.md#external-participants)]
- Respect explicit information needs and choices when selecting an initial or subsequent View. Do not require a summary Lens for a summary View that coordinates independently qualified Projections; synthesis of a new account remains core analysis. [[Summary Views and navigation](decisions/coordinated-views-and-qualified-results.md#allow-summary-views-independently-of-summary-lenses)]

<a id="session-references-and-retained-information"></a>
## Session references and retained information

- Once a session reference is bound to an entity, do not rebind it. Adding inputs, evidence, or results must not change that binding. [[Session references](decisions/transient-analysis-sessions.md#stable-reference-bindings-within-a-session)]
- Retain captured evidence and produced results without mutation by later analysis. Preserve their supporting inputs, method versions, qualifications, and relevant evaluation outcomes; session membership alone does not establish their evidential basis. [[Accumulated results](decisions/transient-analysis-sessions.md#immutable-information-within-an-accumulating-session)]
- Keep observation event identities and clocks separate from program claims. Entity and record references need not reproduce across invocations; equivalent analyses must remain semantically comparable without erasing meaningful differences. [[Session context](decisions/transient-analysis-sessions.md#session-as-the-analysis-and-reference-context)]
- For mechanical analyses, equivalent inputs, methods, requirements, completed evaluations, and presentation choices produce deterministic semantic content, ordering, and presentation apart from session-local references and observation metadata. Cross-session reference spelling is not a determinism requirement. [[Session context](decisions/transient-analysis-sessions.md#session-as-the-analysis-and-reference-context)]
- Unrelated accumulated work does not change a lens's population or meaning. Reuse satisfies declared requirements and expansions; additional information from completing an earlier incomplete evaluation remains distinguishable from changing the requested question. [[Accumulated results](decisions/transient-analysis-sessions.md#immutable-information-within-an-accumulating-session)]
- Under the initial unchanged-input session contract, detected relevant input changes invalidate further investigation. Disclose best-effort detection and non-atomic capture; do not claim comprehensive freshness validation. [[Input stability](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#validity-and-recording)]

- When presenting references that will expire before they can be used as subjects of a subsequent request, make that limitation clear in the surrounding presentation. [[Reference lifetime disclosure](decisions/reference-lifetime-disclosure.md)]

- Keep immutability distinct from retention, disposal, persistence, and availability. Preserve the content and support of retained evidence/results and the meaning of their references; disposal must not silently retarget a reference. This does not prohibit changing Workspace layout, focus, defaults, display selection, or derived current reconsideration status. [[Immutability and retention](decisions/application-specification-boundaries.md#distinguish-immutable-information-from-retention-policy)]

## Retained interpretation

- Apply qualification, evidence attribution, immutable retention, inspection, follow-up, associations, composition, correction and citation-exposure contracts to all [interpretation content](core-concepts.md#interpretation-content), regardless of representation. [[Structured interpretation](decisions/structured-investigram-content.md#permit-structured-interpretation)]

- Select the investigram section of subject inspection through explicit qualified subject associations. Its contents depend on session history; the determinism guarantee applies to the mechanical portion of inspection. Unrelated accumulated work does not broaden the declared inspection population. [[Subject-associated inspection](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#associate-investigrams-with-subjects-and-select-retained-results)]
- Bind investigram references to immutable investigrams within their session. Requests naming a precise investigram reference target that exact investigram, without automatic redirection to a replacement. Lookup handles expose missing or ambiguous matches rather than silently preferring a replacement. Display selection of explicit replacements must use their own references; superseded originals remain inspectable. [[Investigrams](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#represent-retained-interpretation-as-investigrams)] [[Corrections](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#record-explicit-corrections-without-rewriting-earlier-interpretation)]
- Keep a retained investigram composition tree fixed. A subsequent investigation produces a separate result linked to its selected subject through provenance, not additional composition children. Keep both relationships distinct from claims about program structure or logical dependence. [[Composition and investigation provenance](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#distinguish-fixed-composition-from-investigation-provenance)]
- Validate and accept each investigation result with its accompanying corrections as a unit, then retain them atomically. Correction targets must be retained before acceptance; correction relationships take effect on retention. Preserve original investigrams, composition trees, and historical projections. [[Corrections](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#record-explicit-corrections-without-rewriting-earlier-interpretation)]
- Keep correction relationships distinct from composition and investigation provenance. Retain conflicting corrections and disclose revisions and conflicts without silently changing follow-up subjects or treating correction as established truth. [[Corrections](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#record-explicit-corrections-without-rewriting-earlier-interpretation)]
- Construct citation indexes from substantive context actually supplied to the investigator, including correction targets, rather than investigator-reported relevance. Correction eligibility requires the target's complete retained interpretation content, referent information and qualifications supplied during the current evaluation. Exposure records do not establish continued retention, comprehension, or semantic dependence. [[Citation exposure and reconsideration](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#record-citation-exposure-and-derive-reconsideration-status)] [[Structured interpretation](decisions/structured-investigram-content.md#permit-structured-interpretation)]
- Derive and disclose reconsideration status through citations and applicable correction-context exemptions without mutating investigrams or automatically regenerating results. [[Citation exposure and reconsideration](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#record-citation-exposure-and-derive-reconsideration-status)]

## Investigator access

- Transmit repository content to a hosted inference service only after intentional enablement with disclosure of that transmission. Deliberate PostCode-specific provider configuration can constitute enablement; separate per-request confirmation is not required. [[Hosted-service enablement](decisions/investigator-execution-and-evidence-access.md#require-intentional-hosted-service-enablement-and-protect-credentials)]
- When supplying a corrected investigram to the investigator, include the exact requested investigram with an explicit correction notice, replacement accounts, correction reasons and qualifications, and applicable correction chains and conflicting alternatives. Apply this to initial context and subsequent retrieval. Complete correction-context delivery requires both the target's and replacement's own interpretation content, referent information and qualifications, together with correction reasons and qualifications; it does not require their subordinate investigram trees. Record supplied context and its completeness per correction. Disclose bounded omissions; notices, references, summaries and partial delivery do not establish completeness. [[Correction-aware context delivery](decisions/investigrams-and-progressive-investigation.md#record-citation-exposure-and-derive-reconsideration-status)] [[Structured interpretation](decisions/structured-investigram-content.md#permit-structured-interpretation)]
- Provide program evidence to the investigator only through PostCode's subject-based interface and shared evidence acquisition. The subject population is the complete program-access surface; the investigator has no independent filesystem discovery, path-based reads, shell execution, mutation, or web access. [[Investigator evidence access](decisions/investigator-execution-and-evidence-access.md#integrate-interpretation-with-evaluation-and-qualified-evidence-access)]
- Treat repository content supplied to the investigator as evidence to analyze, not instructions to obey. [[Investigator evidence access](decisions/investigator-execution-and-evidence-access.md#integrate-interpretation-with-evaluation-and-qualified-evidence-access)]
- Keep provider credential values outside investigator context, agent-visible commands, output and prompts, investigrams, observations, logs, and assessment records. Report unavailable credential access rather than silently using an exposing fallback. Disclose the credential mechanism's actual access guarantees and limitations. [[Credential boundary](decisions/investigator-execution-and-evidence-access.md#require-intentional-hosted-service-enablement-and-protect-credentials)]

## Generated-output evidence boundary

- Keep PostCode-generated output outside the analyzed repository or exclude it from repository evidence through an explicit, enforced PostCode policy. Do not infer exclusion merely from generic directory names, Git-ignore status, or markings in the content. [[Explicit generated-output evidence boundary](decisions/adopt-identity-evidence-and-observation-constraints.md#exclude-generated-output-through-an-explicit-evidence-boundary)]

## Observation lifecycle and delivery

- Inadequacy feedback records experience; it neither establishes program truth nor changes an investigation, initiates work or applies a correction. Retain associated View/answer, subject, captured basis and investigation context where already available, without reconstruction or further investigation. Missing context does not prevent submission. Context-free feedback must not fabricate project, investigation Session or View context; its observation correlation identifier does not establish investigation context. [[Feedback observations](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#feedback-observations)]
- Submit feedback at its command completion through the selected observation destination under the existing delivery contract. Failure remains visible without invalidating the displayed result or preventing subsequent commands. Keep batches self-contained: include available displayed result artifacts, exact rendered output and associated context rather than references requiring earlier batches or a live store. Missing context stays explicit; no reconstruction is required. Observation history is not restored operational state. [[Feedback observations](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#feedback-observations)]

- Keep observation-sink selection independent of retention, migration, historical-reading, and producer-side cache policy. [[Observation lifecycle policy](decisions/adopt-identity-evidence-and-observation-constraints.md#keep-sink-selection-separate-from-observation-lifecycle-policy)]
- Make observation-delivery failure visible without invalidating an otherwise successfully produced view. [[Observation delivery failure](decisions/adopt-identity-evidence-and-observation-constraints.md#preserve-a-successful-view-when-observation-delivery-fails)]

- Choose research capture for the research question, with automatic recording where retrospective reconstruction would be unreliable. Ordinary non-research use need not produce or export research records. No comprehensive event taxonomy or field set is required for every use. Preserve or reference enough context for the intended interpretation, disclose relevant limits and deliver resulting research records to the designated research destination under the generated-output boundary. Do not treat ephemeral references as retained evidence. [[Research capture](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#research-capture)]

- Keep research collection distinct from architectural provenance, qualification, exact-answer continuity and investigation attribution. When recording Views or their changes, preserve or reference the composition, contributing bases and surrounding context needed for the research question; do not imply that displayed output establishes a complete captured basis. Required local investigation lineage is independent of research export. - The delivery-failure rule also applies to non-View resolution results and usage reports: expose observation-delivery failure without invalidating an otherwise delivered result. When recording these interactions, describe actual output, usage and outcome without a fabricated View or view-produced event. Choosing not to record is not a failed delivery. ## Source evidence and human understanding

- Human understanding must not depend on programming-language source or syntax. Apply this to Views, generated explanations, evidence detail, qualifications, diagnostics and non-View reports. An interface's declared purpose and consumption contract determine its audience, not its encoding or caller identity. Public CLI text and JSON share the human-facing contract even when invoked by an agent. [[Human output boundary](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#human-output-boundary)]
- Assertions, labels and evidence embedded in source artifacts may be presented when intelligible without source-language literacy, preserving provenance and epistemological status. Identifiers, paths, references and mappings may support traceability. Do not suppress useful information solely because it originated in source or resembles code, or hide material qualifications to simplify it. [[Human output boundary](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#human-output-boundary)]
- Do not routinely display programming-language source as supporting evidence. Any display of source as source must serve a specific purpose that does not require the human to decipher it, and for which an intelligible description or reference would not suffice. Such display does not replace disclosure of an unmet architectural information need. [[Human output boundary](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#human-output-boundary)]
- Preserve source acquisition, captured evidence and implementation mappings for analysis and authorized agent access. Evidence attributed or delivered for a claim comes from its supporting capture, not a later filesystem read. Machine evidence delivery is distinct from human presentation; existing investigator access restrictions remain in force. [[Captured evidence](decisions/adopt-identity-evidence-and-observation-constraints.md#present-source-evidence-from-captured-analysis-input)] [[Human output boundary](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#human-output-boundary)]
- When PostCode recognizes an inadequate representation, disclose the limitation and preserve independently intelligible qualified information. Do not present inadequate content as an adequate answer. Express material qualifications through intelligible computational or architectural concepts; inability to do so is a representation limit, not permission to omit them. [[Representation limits](decisions/human-facing-architectural-representations.md#representation-limits-and-feedback)]
- Holding or passing resolved core support internally is not human disclosure. Classify what each Presentation actually exposes, including expansions and exports; new exposed fields require boundary coverage independently of research recording. Source-bearing qualified content goes through its appropriate classified View. Non-View reports carry bounded request, reason and reference information; they must not become an unclassified source-export path. [[Qualified construction and disclosure](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#qualified-construction)] [[Actual exposure](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#actual-exposure)]
- When research captures human output, distinguish actual exposure, attribution disclosures, displayed source, recognized representation limits and submitted inadequacy feedback as applicable to the collected material. Detail access or source display does not itself establish inadequacy; machine acquisition is not human presentation. Preserve the context needed to interpret the record without treating syntax classification as a semantic adequacy test or reinterpreting historical observations. [[Actual exposure](decisions/research-question-driven-observation.md#actual-exposure)]
