# Common analysis contributions

This describes the implemented common contribution mechanism under `src/lib/contributions/`. It implements the mechanism, vertical slice, mechanical families and agentic/caller migration in the [structured architectural accounts plan](../plans/structured-architectural-accounts.md) and is governed by [common analysis contributions](../decisions/common-analysis-contributions.md), [association organization and exposure](../decisions/association-organization-and-exposure.md), [qualified answers over contributions](../decisions/qualified-answers-over-contributions.md) and [structured architectural explanation](../decisions/structured-architectural-explanation.md).

## Current integration status

Module discovery is the first real producer. Each Session's record store has one contribution ledger paired with it and released with it. When a module evaluation is first recorded, each module's identifying finding is contributed once, under the discovery method, and a separately attributed deterministic policy uses that finding as the module's identification. A module record and its common Entity resolve to each other; equivalent recomputation recognizes the established finding and adds nothing, so a withdrawn identification is not renewed. A mechanical qualification can name only a Claim context of its own producing method.

Export evaluation is the second real producer. Each module's export finding is contributed once per distinct result, under the export method, and a separately attributed policy uses it as information about that module's exports. Provider contributions are identified by their provider key, so their identity does not depend on which evaluation happened to establish them first.

The internal exports answer is the first consumer. It selects the discovery method's own identifying finding and the export method's own finding from the ledger, and captures each use's standing and qualified displacement context in the retained answer. Fixed support retains each displacing declaration, its producer and owner/declaration qualifications, grounds evidence and supporting content. Export presentation labels these organizing judgments separately from the mechanical finding's qualification; raw evidence bodies stay in internal support, while optional source detail exposes declaration locations. Captured producing-context concerns are shown under the displacing production, with exact cause references, routes, grounds and qualifications in the structured View. The warning does not reverse the displacement, reinstate an earlier use or qualify the mechanical finding as erroneous. Earlier exact answers keep their captured concerns. This is read-only assembly and records no producer exposure. A finding stays selected after a use of it is displaced; a fresh answer then shows the displacement with its grounds, and an earlier answer keeps what it captured. The answer fails if a finding it needs was never contributed, so a producer that bypasses the ledger is detected.

The implementation routes `summarize`, `explain`, `decompose` and `examine`, together with the M2 contribution Lenses, through the common dialog. Application configuration select the common hosted protocol; verification of this migration is offline only. The earlier M2 live trials do not verify the revised M3 guidance.

The caller path accepts exact contribution, item/component and result-designation subjects. Inspection captures explicit associated uses and their standing, or an exact selected contribution, without running a dialog. A mixed inspection retains a separate native answer alongside its common selection. Name lookup also considers accumulated identifying contributions, including historical names with their standing disclosed; a match is not a claim of canonical naming or a complete program-wide Entity population. The application returns complete fixed content and precise identities. CLI paging/continuations and compact aliases have retired. Exact replay and detached arrangement use the captured selection and support.

The accepted [attempt/answer decision](../decisions/investigation-attempts-and-answer-outcomes.md) applies uniformly to every investigation operation. Failed attempts retain their kind/code/diagnostic in the Session ledger and request reporting, including an unconfigured attempt with no invented agent. Compatible completed attempts remain reusable; communication/configuration failures remain eligible for retry. Empty selections can have the same answer identity despite distinct attempts. A failed fresh attempt leaves a previous designation and its fulfillment intact. Exact presentation has no attempt report; selected owners still carry their applicable reports and limitations. Full production histories remain attempt records rather than duplicated answer support: an answer captures selected evidence, producer/origin/report and independent standing/cause snapshots.

Operation reuse fingerprints the complete definition and fixed follow-up catalog as well as its declared method. Common acquisitions have explicit work-attribution entries and actual dispatch histories. A contribution response refused or omitted by the delivery boundary is marked withheld, even when an omission notice is subsequently dispatched; only the actual bounded content enters exposure. Merely assembling or displaying an answer creates no producer exposure. The unreachable tree/correction evaluator, selection, context and presentation helpers and their old protocol are removed; shared evidence, transport, credentials and usage remain.

## Mechanical families and retained inputs

Module expansions, dependencies, repository organization and dependency-organization
expansions retain their provider statements through the common acceptance boundary.
Symbol and group identification resolve the provider's scoped native identity before
allocating a common Entity. Group roots retain explicit anonymity; symbols retain
language names and roles. Export routes, documentation associations and recorded
assertions, composition, dependency occurrences and recognition coverage, containment,
placement and group properties keep their existing provider records and qualifications.
An addressable relationship or assertion does not become an Entity. Findings whose
subject is a non-Entity record remain addressable through their owning contribution;
no filler identification or invented Entity is needed to retain them.

The native wrapper checks each statement against its retained provider record. It
attributes the finding to its own Claim-context method, including an existing composite
method where the provider carries inherited qualification. Recorded documentation
retains assertion status. A separately attributed deterministic policy associates
Entity-subject findings with their explicit subjects. Provider keys prevent equivalent
recomputation from adding another use or renewing a withdrawn one. Native record IDs
remain implementation references, mapped to common identities under the provider's
rule and scope; names never act as identity keys. Reverse mapping treats a native key
only as a candidate locator: the complete provider/rule/scope/key tuple, Entity kind
and implemented modality must match the retained provider record. The same check
governs inspection, module-operation eligibility (including follow-up options) and
dialog module references. A distinct native identity remains independently
inspectable without receiving the candidate module’s mechanical content. Mapping
is identity bookkeeping and creates no exposure.

Provider-native identity claims require host-established namespace authority at the
ledger's atomic acceptance boundary, including reassertions of an existing native
tuple. Ordinary producers reuse the common Entity ID or introduce a separately
identified Entity without a native tuple. Being mechanical, naming a provider's
method, or submitting an object that resembles a credential grants no authority.
This restriction does not limit another producer's qualified additions,
identifications, challenges or withdrawals concerning that common Entity.

The host installs a default-deny authorization policy when constructing a ledger and
supplies an opaque credential only when opening an authorized provider production.
The credential is kept in a ledger-local weak map keyed by that exact production
handle, removed at conclusion, and omitted from retained records and delivery.
Another production in the same acceptance pool cannot borrow it. The built-in
TypeScript and repository-layout adapters share a host credential; the Session
policy additionally requires the exact native tuple of a retained provider record,
so it cannot introduce a different provider, rule, scope or native key. No growing
module-global authority registry is used. This is a host adapter boundary, not a
permission inferred from producer-declared metadata or the hosted submission schema.

Forward and reverse mapping both require the expected Entity kind and implemented
modality. A host-authorized but incompatible mapping therefore cannot become a
module-operation subject. Contribution acceptance advances to
`postcode/contributions@2`; the native tuple and common-ID formulas are unchanged.


Organization providers can emit new record IDs when a requested module requirement
set changes without changing the finding. Their contribution keys therefore compare
the statement, its qualification and relevant fixed population, excluding incidental
evaluation identity. Dependency-organization keys also normalize their supporting
placement claims. The original exact provider record remains in the established item;
equivalent later records resolve to that same item.

Compiler-derived findings are obtained independently from source. Repository layout
uses captured repository evidence. Placement and group properties consume a fixed
retained module population; dependency organization consumes retained relationships,
occurrences attached to relationships, and the placement, containment, group and artifact-placement
findings it reads. It does not receive group properties, documentation or non-edge requests
merely because they are retained in the supplied evaluations. Those derivations record exact item deliveries
without claiming an evolving inventory or delivery through an organizing association.
Mapping identities and constructing answers record no delivery. Internal computation
within a provider pass does not manufacture an extra invocation or acquired input.
Every declared retained input must already resolve to a contributed item; missing input
contributions fail before opening a producing execution. Mapping an input to its exact
item does not recursively deliver other findings named in its provider record.

Module, organization, dependency and internal export answers capture their selected
native items, uses and full qualified support. Selection follows the family's existing
population and expansion rules, including partial and unavailable results.
Dependency-organization selections contain only classifications of the answer's own
relationships. Unrelated classifications and their concerns do not enter the snapshot.
Method-specific findings stay selected after a use is displaced. Fresh answers capture changed standing,
exact causes and inconsistency reports; exact answers retain the old snapshot.
Presentation discloses concerns with attributable grounds and qualifications, preserving
precise references in structured output without exporting raw source bodies.

Native and contribution-answer snapshot identity uses exact immutable item/declaration
owners, selected question and content references, and captured derived status. Literal
selection parameters remain verbatim; Session normalization applies only to known
reference positions, never serialized prose or qualification text. Resolved evidence remains in the payload but is not independently re-keyed:
its input capture can include operational output exclusions that do not change the
provider's findings. Collection selection uses deterministic provider ordering, and
native-use lookup indexes each selected Entity once rather than rescanning all its
associations for every selected item.

The historical-name index now includes native symbol and group identifications as well
as modules. General application subject resolution, agentic evidence delivery, associated inspection
and hosted native-content reference translation use the common model.

## Immutable capture and ownership lifetime

The program store owns an `ImmutableCapture` boundary shared by its contribution
ledger. Store retention, ledger acceptance, contribution-answer capture and production
delivery capture reuse plain immutable subgraphs already owned by that boundary.
`ProgramRecordStore.capture` captures a value without assigning record identity,
accepting a contribution or recording exposure. A standalone ledger has its own
capture boundary, released with that ledger. Independent stores have independent
ownership proofs; sharing grants no reference authority across Sessions.

The boundary copies unknown plain objects/arrays, preserves graph aliases, and freezes
all copied nodes before registering them in its private WeakSet. Neither an arbitrary
caller freeze nor a value received from another store or structured transfer proves ownership. For
accessors or exotic objects it uses native structured cloning for the entire graph,
preserving native aliasing/error behavior and leaving that graph unregistered. There
is no process-wide object registry, ownership marker in delivered data, or callback
from a retained value to its owner.

Selections, standing, displacement and reconsideration causes are still derived at
answer construction and captured as frozen values. Sharing an immutable content item,
declaration or evidence record never turns those snapshots into current-ledger views.
Resolved historical content remains present in the answer graph and can be arranged
after Session close without lookup or analysis. Detached structured delivery uses native cloning; internal pointer sharing does not change wire meaning
or create/remove exposure records. Store collision/reference checks and ledger atomic
acceptance still validate every submission, including already-owned values.

The application explicitly discards the ownership table on close, interruption or
invalidation, even if a facade or old output remains reachable. Late cancellation work
uses ordinary copying and cannot recreate bookkeeping. This releases the table's
capacity as well as its weak keys. Captured answers keep only their immutable data;
they do not keep the table, store or compiler alive. Unclosed internal stores retain
their table for their own lifetime. This change does not optimize canonical identity
construction or comparison allocation, and it does not change answer method versions.

Ordinary deep freezing tracks visited nodes only for that call. Cycles and aliases
therefore remain valid through native fallback and captures after disposal, without
recreating ownership bookkeeping. A shallow-frozen node does not stop traversal into
its descendants; unknown input still receives the full copy-and-freeze treatment.

## State-dependent behavior and its diagram

State-dependent behavior is the one architectural concept supported so far. Three responsibilities are kept apart without separate registries. The concept is agent guidance on recognizing and explaining state-dependent behavior, including when not to apply it. The `state-model` content type is the retained meaning: a bearer that is an Entity or a scoped role, named states, an entry that is a state, unknown or established as none, directed transitions with optional trigger, guard and effect, a coverage claim for states and for transitions, applicability, and any semantics the bounded model cannot express. States and transitions are locally addressable components, not Entities. The diagram type maps a state model to a directed state-transition picture.

The renderer draws one node per supplied state, one edge per supplied transition and an entry marker only for an established entry. It produces an SVG picture, a plain-text form, a legend with every transition's full trigger, guard and effect, and notes stating the exact bearer, entry, coverage, applicability and unsupported semantics. Each model uses its own scoped role or Entity identity; Entity labels and their qualification are captured during answer construction, never looked up by the renderer. Agent-authored labels are neutralized in the picture and shown with visible control characters in text. A model with no states is incompatible, which is a missing-content condition and not a rendering failure.

An explicit state-diagram request has a result contract requiring a designated answer that selects a state model with at least one state. Prose does not meet it, and each standing alternative is assessed on its own selected content.

## Agentic dialog

One dialog is one producing execution with a fresh agent conversation. The outer boundary opens a production for the invocation, supplies initial context, dispatches tool requests, delivers exact change notices, validates the submission with bounded structural repair, and returns one outcome. The agent-communication participant only exchanges messages; provider protocol and credentials stay behind it.

Initial context is acquired through the production like anything requested later: the baseline identifications of a subject Entity, or the exact selected designation, item or contribution. Each delivery includes the owner’s recorded originating invocation, question, subjects, parameters and result contract, separately from the current selection route. A result designation also carries the exact question it designates, even when different from its producer’s question. Whole-owner acquisition provides the same originating context without selecting a current replacement. Explicit `InvocationRequest.context`, including context retained in an invoked option, uses these same acquisitions after the subject responses. Exact duplicate initial subject/context selections are acquired and delivered once, in first-occurrence order (subjects first). All original subject roles and context entries remain in the request. Deduplication uses the complete selected reference, including component and route: item, component, designation, whole-contribution and Entity-baseline selections are not collapsed merely because they reach the same content. Later explicit tool acquisitions remain separate deliveries. Its exact addresses and any selected component remain separate in the dialog request; a component is supplied within its enclosing item. Scope-only selections produce an explicit unavailable response rather than implied content. Context obeys the same response and total-dialog bounds and enters exposure only at dispatch. The hosted option-authoring schema accepts optional `request.context` as a list of direct subject references, distinct from the wrapped investigation subjects. Guidance describes its additional-input purpose and permits omission; it does not require context on every option. A fresh investigation of a repeated question is also supplied the answers currently designated for it.

Tool requests are either program-evidence queries, answered by the existing evidence boundary with exact record references, or acquisitions of retained contributions. Evidence responses carry the host's mapping from module records to common Entities, which is not content. When a requested evidence query obtains mechanical work, that work keeps its own producers, and what it retained reaches the dialog as a separate recorded delivery through the dialog's own production. The evidence page is always delivered when it fits. If the retained finding would push the response past the bound, it is named by address, not delivered, and the response states the omission.

Malformed tool shapes are refused before acquisition; internal defects still propagate. An acquisition is recorded only if its response is actually dispatched to the agent. Dispatch advances the delivery checkpoint before waiting for the response, so a timeout cannot erase context already received. A response withheld by the response bound, a refused request, and anything acquired after the last dispatched exchange are discarded from the recorded history. Before each exchange the dialog delivers an exact notice for each change that would otherwise be a cause, and records it with that exchange.

The dialog distinguishes an accepted contribution with its attempt fulfillment and any reported inability, an unrepaired invalid submission, a limit stop, a dialog that ended without submitting, and communication or configuration failure. Each concludes the production, so nothing can be submitted late. Interruption, invalidation and defects propagate, with final attributed usage reporting from the dialog’s terminal cleanup. These exits accept no late contribution. Supported agentic capabilities use common contributions, explicit organizing declarations and actual-delivery reconsideration. The legacy tree/correction route has been removed. The state-behavior investigation, diagram arrangement and follow-up-option invocation are available through the application route below.

## Responsibilities

A Session-owned **contribution ledger** retains Entities, invocations with their Query results, immutable investigrams and the record of each producing execution. It is a separate component from the program record store. Content items can reference program records such as source evidence and provider Claim contexts; the ledger preserves their meaning and qualification. Native-finding items carry the exact immutable provider statement as their body, validated against its retained record, while source evidence and Claim context remain separately referenced.

Four responsibilities are kept apart:

| Responsibility | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Acceptance | Validates a submitted pool completely, resolves known identities, then allocates and retains everything atomically. It checks ownership, addressability, reference eligibility, qualification presence and content-contract shape. It does not check association reachability, request fulfillment or correctness. |
| Producer input recording | An open **production** is the only route by which a producer acquires retained information. Each acquisition returns qualified content and records what actually arrived, at its delivery position. |
| Derived status | Standing and needs-reconsideration are computed from retained declarations, deliveries and membership under a versioned method. They are never written back into contributions. |
| Outcome and fulfillment | What an attempt returned (nothing, an unrepaired invalid submission, or an accepted contribution) is reported separately from whether the selected answer meets its requested result contract. |

Values returned by read access are immutable or fresh copies, so a caller cannot alter retained declarations, standing or indexes. General identification access returns the standing uses; a method-specific request selects by the method that produced the identifying content, not by the producer of the organizing declaration that uses it, and by default returns every use of that method's findings whatever its standing, with the use and its standing available separately.

Read access to the ledger — identity resolution, name lookup, association and designation selection, status — involves no production and therefore records no exposure. This is the structural basis for answer assembly, browsing and host identity bookkeeping creating no producer exposure.

## Identities

An Entity record carries identity only: its kind, modality, optional provider-native identity and the investigram that introduced it. Names and descriptions are content selected by identification associations, so several can coexist and displacing one does not change what the Entity ID denotes.

A submitted Entity uses a submission-local key. One with a provider-native identity resolves through the combination of provider, identity rule, basis scope and native key; an existing mapping yields the retained Entity and no new identification is required. The same native key under another scope is a different Entity. One without a native identity is always new and must be identified in the same submission. Common IDs are Session-scoped digests, derived from the scoped native tuple or from the introducing investigram and local key. They never equal a native key and establish no equivalence across Sessions or scopes.

An invocation records one request: operation, subjects with roles and order, parameters, explicitly selected context and the requested result contract. Its Query result is a separate identity. An ordinary equivalent request resolves to the existing invocation and result. An explicit fresh request records a new invocation that keeps the repeated question's Query result as its organizing target.

## Contributions and declarations

A submission is a flat pool of investigrams, each belonging to one production; several productions can be accepted in one batch. The pool has no semantic order: a declaration can select content owned by any investigram of the pool, and acceptance prepares everything that could fail before it changes retained state. An investigram owns keyed content items, declarations, follow-up options, illustrations and inconsistency reports. Its content, and each of those parts, needs applicable qualification of its own or from its owner. Agentic qualification always has interpretation status and cannot state a guarantee; mechanical qualification states its status explicitly. Both are attributed to the producing method.

A qualification can name provider Claim-context records. These are the qualified claim's own Claim context as its producing method retains it, not a citation of someone else's. Provider Claim contexts qualify mechanically derived findings, so an agentic qualification cannot name one. Reuse does not need it: referenced content keeps its owner's qualification, a supplied context record can be cited as evidence, and neither transfers a guarantee to the claim that uses it.

Content items have a type from a content catalogue. The type's contract validates shape, resolves any references in the body and reports locally addressable components and content dependencies. Prose and identification types are built in. A Session's catalogue adds the module identification, module exports, native findings and state-model types. A content subject's optional `component` names a locally addressable selector declared by that type's contract, not an arbitrary body field or an Entity. Retained items list valid selectors in `components`; for new submission-local items, production guidance supplies the contract's selector rules. State models declare `state:<key>` and `transition:<key>`, not `unsupported`. Production instructions and the hosted schema share the general explanation and direct agents to omit `component` when no supported selector is available. Domain validation still rejects undeclared selectors and uses the normal bounded repair path.

Declarations are addressed through their owning investigram. An addition or supersession establishes an association at its own address, targeting either an Entity with an identification or topical role, or a Query result. A supersession or withdrawal names exact earlier associations and carries qualified grounds. A supersession's displaced uses must concern the same Entity and role class, or the same Query result; topic keys may differ, and validation does not judge whether two topics mean the same thing. Members are exact content items, and for result designations also other associations, with circular membership rejected. Declarations may reference another owner's content; ownership and qualification stay with the source.

## Exposure and reconsideration

Every delivered item carries its own qualification, referents, evidence and support alongside its owner's qualification; a disclosed excerpt bounds only the body. Content delivered as a member of a particular use always arrives with that use: its grounds, qualification, standing and every displacement already accepted. A bare item can be acquired on its own and is then exposure to no use. A declaration acquired by its address is delivered as the exact use it establishes; only a withdrawal is delivered as bare grounds.

Displacement context delivered with a displaced use is the exact change declaration: its operation, the uses it affects, its grounds and qualification, its owner's provenance and, for a supersession, a reference to the replacement use. It is recorded as delivery of that declaration's grounds. It is not acquisition of the replacement use or of its content. A recipient of A and that context therefore gains no cause when the replacement B is itself displaced later; acquiring B, B's content as a member of B, or a scope listing B does. The delivered explanation keeps its own provenance, so concerns of its producer still reach the recipient by the inherited route. The context also shows the replacement's current standing. If the replacement has itself been displaced, that appears as standing plus a reference to the further change, without its operation or grounds. That is not an exact notice of the further change and does not make it known. A production that receives another contribution's result, whole or as the exact use a declaration established, does receive that declaration with its operation and grounds, and the change is then known to it. Nothing yet invokes secondary work for an open production or returns its result automatically; a host delivers the result through the requesting production.

Malformed acquisition requests are refused whole, before any part is delivered or recorded: a scope must be well formed and name an existing Entity or Query result, and an excerpt bound must be a non-negative whole number. Each recorded delivery is an owned immutable capture, so later changes to a caller's objects cannot rewrite the history.

A production records deliveries of exact content items (complete, or as an excerpt with the delivered and omitted extent of the body, and with the association route when delivered as a particular use), exact associations with the displacements already known, declaration grounds, qualified metadata, acquired inventory scopes (current-only or all-history, with the addresses listed), exact change notices and discovery-only referent handles. Positions are counts of accepted submissions and serve only to order deliveries against changes.

The ledger enumerates associations within a scope in acceptance order. That is repeatable for the same retained information and independent of identifiers, but it depends on arrival order and is not a semantic order. Ordering the content of a mechanical answer belongs to the Lens method's own semantic keys.

Each investigram inherits the deliveries of its own production. Causes are exact change declarations, reached by four routes:

- later displacement of an association use that was received;
- a later addition within an acquired scope, or a later displacement that removes a listed association from a current-only inventory;
- a producing-context cause of the investigram that owns supplied content, declarations or metadata, when that cause was accepted after the delivery;
- later displacement of a member association of a designation, for the designation's own status and for a producer that received the designation. Receiving a designation is not delivery of its members or their content, and none is recorded.

A change accepted at or before a delivery is known context for that delivery. A change is known by every route once that particular change was actually delivered to the production before it submitted: as an exact notice, as a disclosed displacement, as the declaration's grounds, as the exact use it established, or as a concern disclosed with a delivery. Each item, use, declaration and metadata delivery records the exact changes disclosed with it as concerns: its owner's producing-context causes and, for a use, its membership and content-owner causes. The record kinds stay distinct, so a notice is never mistaken for content delivery. A listing that merely reflects a change does not make it known. A production never acquires a cause from its own declarations. Notices, bare references and discovery handles are not content exposure.

Status has separate bearers. An investigram has producing-context causes. An association has its standing, membership causes relative to its own acceptance, its declarer's producing-context causes, and the producing-context causes of other owners whose content it references. A cause reports the current standing of the association its change established.

## Outcomes

The shared producer boundary opens a production, lets the producer acquire inputs and submit, applies bounded structural repair, and concludes the production. An attempt that returns no contribution is retained with its deliveries, its domain failure kind and details, and no investigram. Reuse reconstructs that failure from the production even when the evaluator’s reporting cache is absent. An accepted contribution takes full effect whatever its fulfillment. Structural repair applies to acceptance only: a failure of the fulfillment check after acceptance propagates and never reopens repair. Every terminal exit ends the production. It stays open only while a permitted structural repair continues; a producer defect or an unexpected failure during acceptance validation propagates unchanged and discards the open production, so nothing can be submitted late.

Fulfillment has two distinct subjects. An attempt's fulfillment is assessed over the answers that attempt supplied: the designations it declared for its invocation's Query result and any exact existing designations it reported as its answer, which must belong to that result. An earlier answer that still stands does not fulfill a later attempt that neither designated nor returned an answer. Production instructions and the hosted report schema explain that own designations already count, so repeating them in `report.answer` is optional. Explicit report entries name retained owning investigram handles and declaration keys, or submission-local investigram keys and declaration keys; each must be an add or supersede designation of the invocation’s Query result. Invocation and Query-result handles are not investigram references. Reporting an existing designation neither creates a new designation nor changes its standing or reconsideration causes. A withdrawn or superseded designation remains eligible to be returned: that is neither endorsement nor re-adoption. Making its content current again requires a new declaration. Fulfillment alternatives capture their designation standing and exact displacing changes with their operations when assessed; a returned withdrawn designation can fulfill the attempt while the question separately has no current answer. A report-only return may submit an empty `investigrams` array. Each supplied investigram must contribute content, declarations or qualified metadata; qualification alone is insufficient. An optional adequacy judgment may instead be retained as a qualified prose item without a new answer designation. Production instructions and the hosted array description state these alternatives. The Query result as a whole is assessed separately, against every current designation or an explicitly selected alternative, and distinguishes a result that never had a designation from one whose designations were all displaced.

## Deliberate limits at this stage

Inventories are delivered whole; bounded or ranked listings with explicit coverage are not implemented. Selected-content baselines and fixed Projection support are implemented for the new application route. Precise retained contribution IDs and fixed inspection content are available through the application; persistence is outside this delivery. Status derivation invalidates its memoization after each acceptance. The M2 measurement records a bounded accumulating Session exercise; it is not a statistical performance assessment.

## Application route

The representative Lenses below are answered from contributions and visible in the application catalogue with their subject signatures and result contracts. They have no command-line spelling or menu. The migrated `summarize`, `explain`, `decompose` and `examine` Lenses also use contributions through the direct application; investigation CLI routes are retired.

| Lens | Subject | Request | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `state-behavior` | One discovered module, by name or reference | The state-behavior operation with the state-diagram result contract | A contribution answer |
| `follow-up` | One retained contribution, by its exact identity, with the option key as a parameter | The investigation the option proposes, under the option's own result contract | A contribution answer |

Evaluation resolves the request to its invocation and Query result. An ordinary request reuses a retained outcome for that question, whether it was accepted or ended in a retained failure, and runs no dialog. An accepted attempt takes precedence over an earlier failed attempt for reuse reporting; this does not prioritize or suppress any standing answer alternative. Communication and configuration failures are not reusable. An explicit `fresh` request always runs a new producing attempt; it is refused for mechanical Lenses. The investigation Lenses accept it through the application API.

Invoking a follow-up option includes its exact owner and key in the question identity, as directed in the task follow-up for review finding R4-F1. Distinct options remain distinct questions even with identical operation, subjects and result contract. An ordinary invocation can reuse that same option’s outcome; a fresh invocation adds answers only to that option’s Query result. The defining option and its meaning are retained in fixed answer support and identified in attempt reporting. The option must name a supported operation and satisfy that operation's subject signature, otherwise the request is refused without running anything. Follow-up requests undergo prospective Presentation and supported-expansion checks before evaluation, so an already incompatible request cannot run a dialog or accept organizing changes. Production instructions enumerate the supported operations, their subject signatures and optional result-contract identities from the invocation catalogs. The option itself is delivered to the dialog as substantive input, and the exact selected content it names is the dialog's subject.

The answer is a retained record that selects the current designated answers of the question: every standing alternative, in stable enumeration with no preference. It captures each alternative's content items with their own and their owners' qualification and producer, its membership and producing-context causes, unresolved inconsistency reports, the requirements of the requested result contract its content does not meet, and the follow-up options it offers. It also captures displaced designations with their grounds and the fulfillment of the request as a whole. The fixed support retains the exact member-use graph, owner and declaration qualifications, already-displaced members with qualified grounds, precise item/use conflicts, source reports, selected owners’ illustrations with their qualified exact inputs, recursively required item support, and resolved program evidence and Claim contexts. Shared support records are captured once per answer, and unrelated Session positions do not enter answer identity. Historical designations retain their original content as fixed support. Because the record carries its selected content, exact presentation follows only that record and needs neither the ledger nor a producer. A later declaration yields a different answer on the next request and leaves this one unchanged.

Trial support under `scripts/structured-accounts/support/` turns a supplied contribution answer into explanation with pictures: for each alternative its qualifications, prose, a diagram for every state model that supports one, or the reason none can be drawn. Qualification groups identify exact sources: selected content and uses, supporting items, bearer identifications, displacements, reconsideration changes, and inconsistency reports. Each group separates its source’s statements from its owner’s qualification and report. Result-contract shortfalls remain attributed to the selected designation. Fixed support captures identifying names and their origins through each content type’s identifying-name contract, including mechanical module names and generated handles; their source bodies retain detailed handle provenance. Diagrams use those captured names or handles for each Entity bearer, or the model’s scoped role; exact Entity references stay in the structured content, not the rendered labels. Each selected item not rendered is explicitly listed by exact address and type; every incompatible state model also carries its bearer, full model and reason, including when other models in that alternative can be drawn. When state labels collide, including after truncation, consistent state mnemonics distinguish nodes, entry, text and transition endpoints, with a full state legend. The arrangement states when alternatives are shown separately and when only some can be drawn, and reports the no-current-answer condition with the withdrawal history. Each standalone SVG/text diagram identifies the trial answer, its alternative's position and exact designation, and the selected state-model item's exact address. Multiple alternatives carry their count, equal standing and unreconciled status; the position implies no preference. Each diagram visibly carries only that alternative's supplied interpretation status and applicable owner limitations. The SVG includes the existing entry, coverage, applicability and unsupported-content notes and explicitly refers to the companion text artifact with the same basename for the full state and transition legend. Word wrapping preserves the supplied text without truncation. This rendering-only disclosure does not change the structured answer. Arrangement has no ledger/store access and adds no program claim.

Application reporting carries the producing attempt’s outcome, its own fulfillment and producer report separately from fulfillment of the current selected answer. It also captures the attempt’s supplied designation addresses and standing, even when no result contract applies. Application attempt reporting identifies withdrawn versus superseded designations and explains that returning them does not re-adopt them. Cached reporting preserves the standing it captured when assessed; a later assessment is a new observation, not a mutation of that report. A fresh attempt can report inability and no answer supplied while an older standing answer remains fulfilled. Invocation-only outcomes stay outside that answer’s identity. Selected owners’ reports remain available through exact presentation; attempt diagnostics and reports without a selected answer are delivered only in application reporting. An exact empty answer describes the retained selection, independently of why a particular attempt failed. When no agent is configured the request returns an answer with no alternatives and carries the domain unavailability code and diagnostic in reporting; nothing is fabricated.

## Hosted transport

The hosted OpenAI transport is generic over a typed dialog protocol: two functions, one to request information and one to submit, plus the structural translation of that protocol's reference fields. Credentials, routes, streaming, usage reporting, failure classification and exchange capture are shared and unchanged. The original investigator protocol and the contribution dialog protocol are two such protocols; an agent identity records the contribution protocol in its configuration.

The contribution protocol follows the [reference transport decision](../decisions/investigator-reference-transport.md). Identities travel as short handles from one private per-dialogue table. Translation is structural: a string at a designated reference key is an identity, and everything else is literal, including names, local keys, prose, labels, qualification text and scope descriptions that happen to contain an identity. An embedded program-evidence response is translated by the existing typed record traversal. An unknown handle in a reply stays explicitly unresolvable, so the dialog reports it unavailable or acceptance rejects it. An unparseable submission is still a submission, subject to validation and bounded repair.


## Presentation and hosted reference preservation

An offered follow-up option carries its own qualification alongside its exact metadata
address, meaning, operation and result contract. Its owner's qualification is separately
attributed rather than replacing the narrower option qualification. Trial arrangement and
qualification groups preserve this distinction from the answer's captured support, including
when presenting an earlier answer after later history or Session close.

The hosted contribution codec translates references inside known content bodies according
to their type. In particular, `module-exports.body.exports` uses the same dialogue-local
handles as its evidence references, and copied handles decode on submission. Reason text,
qualification text and opaque bodies remain literal. These translations do not acquire
content or alter exposure, acceptance or historical answers.
