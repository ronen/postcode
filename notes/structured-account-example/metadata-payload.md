# Complete payload: immutable contributions and entity projections

Updated 2026-11-12. The current [consolidation](../structured-architectural-accounts-consolidation.md)
is exercised by both partitions. This tour replaces the earlier whole-own-investigram
correction/delivery description; it is not a product schema.

## Submission and retained ownership

Read [children first result](children/first-result.json), [follow-up](children/followup-result.json)
and their [ledger](children/context-and-bindings.json). The [mixed](first-result.json)
version preserves the comparison with broader source qualifications.

| Part | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `queryInvocation` / `queryResult` | Particular invocation and separately allocated result entity. |
| `investigrams` | Immutable qualified contributions with local keys resolved on acceptance. |
| `compositionRoots` / `children` | Forest packaging of newly submitted bundles; no special replacement tree. |
| `content` | Keyed architectural interpretation, including explicit entity introduction. |
| `referents` | Things referred to; not necessarily targets of an identifying/describing claim. |
| `associationSpecifications` | Qualified additions, supersessions and withdrawals targeting entity/role categories. |
| `followupOptions` / `presentations` | Exact-owner qualified guidance, separate from architectural content. |
| `qualifications` / `evidence` | Own scope and support across content/metadata; narrower entries can add limits. |

Basis and request/generating provenance remain in the ledger and qualified delivered
wrappers. New architectural entity IDs are bound from descriptions; the query-result
identity is allocated for Q. References do not declare extra entities. Each output
has meaningful substance, but it need not contain new content if its declarations
organize delivered content meaningfully.

## Delivery and exposure

A request's `deliveries` select entities plus exact associations, item excerpts or
options. The model expands chosen association members and required content dependencies,
then captures original qualification, basis, evidence and source-query provenance.
The exposure index records selected context entities/results plus exact association-use
routes; reconsideration uses those exact routes, not other information sharing an entity.
Result projections also preserve the invocation's declared meaning, scope and parameters. Bare IDs and human browsing do not create exposure.

Q2's subject/context entity is RESULT-Q1. Its option address remains
ADMISSION-1/concurrent-callers-option, and its architectural focus is S. Those are
three different roles. This request deliberately delivers the declarations and full
members it will supersede. Ordinary option delivery need not do that; the historical
owner's complete metadata is no longer an eligibility unit.

Fresh projections disclose relevant qualified displacement reasons, whose references
do not themselves deliver every old/new member. Captured answers remain unchanged.
Generated [lifecycle reports](children/lifecycle-report.json) show both fresh historical
admission access and selected new admission access, with exact source provenance.
There is no automatic current-definition selection or whole-source superseded status.

## Assessment and limits

The same mechanisms now handle initial identification, expanded descriptions, result
redesignation, old-content reuse and withdrawal. Original ownership remains visible.
The main new design pressure is delivery and reconsideration precision, not adding a
mutable canonical entity model.

The [verification guide](README.md#verification) lists checks. Same-target displacement,
full declaration/member eligibility and one initial result designation are explicit
provisional choices. Exact designation statuses separate displacement from membership and
generating-context concerns; shared entity/item identity alone is not a cause. Required dependency delivery for
arbitrary excerpts, declaration challenge, source-aware exemptions and reassessment
remain decisions to make before the plan. No governing/product files were changed.
