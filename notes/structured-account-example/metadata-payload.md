## Delivery and exposure

A request's `deliveries` select entities plus exact associations, item excerpts or
options. The model expands chosen association members and required content dependencies,
then captures original qualification, basis, evidence and source-query provenance.
Association exposure records exact supplied content and association-use routes; entity/result
IDs organize access, not blanket exposure. Result projections also preserve the invocation's
meaning, scope and parameters. Bare subject IDs and human browsing create no exposure.

Inventory exposure is separate: an explicit availability request records scope/coverage,
entries and actual delivery position. The [addition probe](additions.md) lists mechanical
association addresses/roles, without delivering member content or producing-investigation
reasons. Requesting a listed association later creates content exposure. By-reference descriptions and
fixed result membership do not imply inventory acquisition. Descriptions selected by a declared
scope, rather than exact references, also record that selection guarantee and position; they
need not be preceded by a separately displayed inventory. Substantive listing summaries
would themselves require qualified content exposure.

Q2's subject/context entity is RESULT-Q1. Its option address remains
ADMISSION-1/concurrent-callers-option, and its architectural focus is S. Those are
three different roles. This request deliberately delivers the declarations and full
members it will supersede. Ordinary option delivery need not do that; the historical
owner's complete metadata is no longer an eligibility unit.

Fresh projections disclose relevant qualified displacement reasons, whose references
do not themselves deliver every old/new member. The supplied declaration/reason is itself an
exact exposure for later displacement, distinct from its referenced members and the displacement acquisition instruction. Captured answers remain unchanged.
Generated [lifecycle reports](children/lifecycle-report.json) show both fresh historical
admission access and selected new admission access, with exact source provenance.
There is no automatic current-definition selection or whole-source superseded status.
Later matching inventory entries can affect an investigator's earlier selection opportunity;
additions do not affect every recipient of an entity's description. Required exact displacement
and inherited-cause disclosures remain; unrelated addition reasons are not automatically sent.

## Assessment and limits

The same mechanisms now handle initial identification, expanded descriptions, result
redesignation, old-content reuse and withdrawal. Original ownership remains visible.
The main new design pressure is delivery and reconsideration precision, not adding a
mutable canonical entity model.

The [verification guide](README.md#verification) lists checks. Same-target displacement,
one initial result designation and accurate qualified deliveries are explicit provisional choices.
Complete target-support acquisition before displacement is an investigator instruction; mechanical
completeness enforcement is deferred. Reuse and earlier-query redesignation likewise have no prior-
delivery gate; reference integrity and accurate records remain enforced. Exact designation statuses separate displacement from membership and
generating-context concerns; shared entity/item identity alone is not a cause. General inventory
coverage, required dependency delivery for arbitrary excerpts and declaration standing need
bounded contracts. Investigator-directed propagation, targeted suppression, reassessment/clearance
and automatic scheduling are deferred. They do not need new payload fields in this capability.
No governing/product files were changed.


Delivery snapshots record their retained-state position, and accepted contributions record
acceptance order. Known direct and indirect concerns accompany fresh projections. They do
not become new warnings for that recipient; missing disclosure is rejected instead. Earlier
snapshots and warnings remain unchanged, and a later refresh is not reassessment. The
[lifecycle report](children/lifecycle-report.json) includes explicit timing probes and the
complete main ordering, including excerpt/updated-answer consumers and the competing copy.
