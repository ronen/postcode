# Explorative capabilities

Status: working note; durable but non-governing

**Explorative:** a capability whose results meet PostCode's ordinary requirements
for evidence and qualification, but whose usefulness for human understanding
has not yet been established. It is identified as explorative so formative use
can reveal where its results help, add little, invite misleading
interpretations, or need refinement.

The term describes uncertainty about a capability's usefulness, not weaker
confidence in its individual results. Its claims still require the ordinary
evidence, provenance and method, scope, epistemological status or guarantee,
limitations, and evaluation outcomes. Formative use does not itself strengthen
a claim or establish an interpretation.

Visibly identifying such a capability is a candidate product choice.
`[explorative]` is a possible compact label, but neither that wording nor a
requirement to display it has been adopted. If a plan calls for consistent
visibility across presentations, or otherwise makes this distinction part of
their shared semantics, consider promoting the term to
[`core-concepts.md`](../docs/core-concepts.md) through an accepted decision.
That decision should define what the status qualifies and what, if anything,
presentations must disclose. The status should not be confused with a claim's
epistemological qualification or an evaluation outcome.
