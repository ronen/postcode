# Admission, follow-up and association supersession: walkthrough

Updated: 2026-11-12. Exploratory example of the [current proposal](structured-architectural-accounts-proposal.md),
not adopted behavior. [Complete payloads](structured-account-example/README.md) now conform
to entity projections, query-result entities and association declarations. The Cockatiel
admission omission is staged, not a new live investigator discovery.

## 1. Q1 produces information, entities and a result designation

Q1 is a particular invocation about policy kind P, grounded in the mechanical module M.
P and M are distinct seeded identities with qualified identifying information; discovering
them end to end is not claimed. Q1 receives a qualified projection of P's orientation.
Its exposure index records P, not the whole orientation investigram as a context unit.

PostCode allocates RESULT-Q1 and resolves Q1's explicit introductions of Closed, Open and
Half-open S. No registration/promotion threshold or matching by name. S's category identity
is distinct from a particular breaker role, execution or hypothetical failure mode.

The children partition returns INTRO-1, DEFINITIONS-1 and ADMISSION-1. The mixed partition
returns ADMISSION-1 with all the same initial items. Either way each item is associated
explicitly. An addition specification identifies S using `half-open-description`; another
describes P using `admission-cases`. A result association targets RESULT-Q1 and designates
those exact declarations. The bundle tree records original composition, not entity scope
or a promise that all descendants will accompany every later projection.

The admission cases deliberately claim coverage while omitting waiting during an occupied
trial. Without that coverage claim, the absence might simply be partiality. The initial
name/definition remain qualified content, not authoritative properties of S.

ANSWER-Q1 captures the original selected content, declaration membership and qualifications.
Later declarations cannot change that captured answer. The stored Q1 result designation
also remains exact, even after a different association becomes available for RESULT-Q1.

## 2. Selecting a follow-up option supplies RESULT-Q1 information

ADMISSION-1 owns `concurrent-callers-option`. The fixture stipulates a supported follow-up
operation; the request identifies that exact metadata owner/key while its contextual subject
is RESULT-Q1. Its resolved architectural focus is S. A direct subject request without an
origin would not imply that the user came from ADMISSION-1 or ANSWER-Q1.

Q2 explicitly receives RESULT-Q1's initial designated members and the qualified option. This
is full selected-result delivery in this example, not a default to transmit every entity's
history. The record materializes exact declarations/items with their owner, source qualifications,
evidence references, captured basis and query provenance. The projection also supplies Q1's declared question, subjects, parameters and scope. The option excerpt alone would not
establish eligibility to displace a result association. Human browsing alone is not exposure.

The option promises qualified investigation, not a particular diagram. The investigator
chooses state and interaction content because it is useful. A different explicit diagram
promise would need compatible data plus successful rendering, with unmet outcomes disclosed.

Q-observer is an authored intervening consumer of the initial result. It provides a concrete
later result whose exposure can become questionable after Q2's declarations, without copying
or replacing Q1's original bundle.

## 3. Q2 contributes trial content and two explicit supersessions

Q2 introduces scenario roles R, E-trial, E-caller and waiting relationship W. In the children
partition ROLE-DEFINITIONS-1 identifies these and TRIAL-1 describes the behavior. The mixed
partition keeps them together. Their scope remains explicit; the trial is not a real observed
execution, and this recovery model is scenario-bound, not silently a model of every P instance.

The behavioral content distinguishes entering Half-open, reserving the sole trial slot,
waiting, classification, transition, release/recheck, then refusal or ordinary work. Explicit
`causedBy` links express the causal chain; sequence position does not. An error not matched by
the failure policy is recorded as recovery success. Reentrant listener requests, non-default
sampling and other stated exclusions remain outside the behavioral scope.

ADMISSION-2 is now simply another contribution bundle containing the expanded admission item.
It does not duplicate unchanged definitions or inspection prose. It declares:

> Supersede ADMISSION-1's `admission-cases` association for P/describes with this new item,
> because the earlier coverage claim omitted waiting and its subsequent admission recheck.

Separately, Q2's root declares a new association for RESULT-Q1, superseding its earlier
result designation. It includes the original definitions/orientation/inspection associations
and the new admission association. Existing items keep their owners and qualification.
RESULT-Q2's own initial association designates Q2's contributions, including the redesignation.
Redesignation is optional and limited to the given result/question. Other results containing
affected associations receive notices or membership concerns, not automatic rewritten answers.
No investigram is globally superseded. There is no replacement-owned tree or K record.

ADMISSION-1's other associations remain; RESULT-Q1's original designation remains historically
accessible. New names/descriptions are not inferred merely from supersession. The original
follow-up option remains with ADMISSION-1; ADMISSION-2 does not repeat it, since Q2 already
answers that question. No independent guidance lifecycle or automatic transfer is introduced.

## 4. Selection, completeness and reconsideration

An exact lookup identifies the requested declaration/item. Fresh access can include displacement
notices and qualified new alternatives; references in those notices are not delivery of all
old/new content. The fixture's new eligibility rule requires receipt of the entire targeted
declaration and its designated members with qualification, not every item in its owner.
It rejects identifier-only or qualification-stripped targets. This proposed rule replaces an
existing governing requirement and must be adopted explicitly before product work.

Q1 remains the producer of its original assertions and has no incoming-exposure warning:
its P orientation was not the admission association it later created. RESULT-Q1's original
designation receives a membership concern about admission and separately records its successor.
The new designation receives neither its own creation cause nor the old admission cause.
Q2 is exempt from its own declaration causes. No warning changes an exact capture.

Q-observer received the original result designation and inherits its concerns. Q-excerpt
received only the admission item via its P association: it receives the admission cause,
not the redesignation cause of an answer it never received. Q-updated receives the new
designation; it does not inherit warnings merely because its context entity is also RESULT-Q1.
The report shows this checkpoint before subsequent independent expansion.

Withdrawal of a later P association does not flag Q1 or other uses merely sharing its item.
Actual delivery time now distinguishes known context from subsequent change. A projection
received after displacement carries that notice; the known cause does not newly flag its
recipient. This also applies to indirect designation/producer concerns and new designation
membership, without clearing the historical source's warnings. Missing required disclosure
is a delivery defect. Notices do not supply all replacement content or prove its adequacy.
A query that acquired the item before the change can still be flagged even if it finishes
later or refreshes afterward. General reassessment and clearance remain unimplemented.

## 5. Expansion without a factual-error claim

Q-expansion delivers S's initial identifying association and trial behavior through R. It
produces a fuller category identification distinguishing the breaker state from execution
progress, and supersedes the original identifying association without alleging it false.
Both variants use qualification appropriate to this new identification. The waiting example
is a separately scoped related-content link to TRIAL-1, not a general behavior claim. Original
source excerpts retain their old limitations; new identification is grounded at the pinned basis.

This can affect later analysis. Q1 was never exposed to newly introduced S, but RESULT-Q1
includes S's identifying declaration. A result-membership cause therefore reaches RESULT-Q1,
and a consumer exposed to RESULT-Q1 can inherit it. The checker separately asserts that Q1's
exposure history does not acquire S retrospectively.

Two independently supported supersessions can target the same association. Both are retained
and shown as competing declarations. The fixture makes no latest-wins truth judgment. Whether
an application should present a primary candidate is a later selection-policy decision.

## 6. Reuse and withdrawal with no new content items

Q-reuse returns REUSE-1 with no new content items. Its addition associates the exact original
Half-open description with P/describes, explaining the limited policy-related use. Its own
result includes that declaration. Content remains owned by the original defining investigram;
REUSE-1 owns only the new qualified organizing assertion.

Q-withdraw then withdraws that broad policy-indexing association without replacement. It
explains why that use is being withdrawn. It does not alter the original text, erase its
history, or withdraw its identifying use for S. The source description's earlier explicit
supersession remains a different declaration with its own cause. WITHDRAW-1's result contains
the withdrawal declaration, making a declaration-only answer meaningful without filler prose.

The order matters: Q-reuse receives the original description after EXPANSION-1, including
its displacement notice. It therefore has no new expansion cause, nor does Q-withdraw inherit
that known cause through REUSE-1. The later competing declaration still affects earlier
recipients. Q-competing itself receives the expansion notice and is not flagged by the
already-known declaration it competes with. Competition and displaced standing remain visible.

These are deliberately bounded organizational examples, not semantic judgments a schema can
certify. Reasons normally remain inline; additional exact evidence/content references can
supply grounds. No special “revise the reason” operation is needed. Later findings have their
own provenance rather than retroactively becoming the earlier investigation's evidence.

## 7. Why keep the children comparison?

Independent association targeting and item delivery now work in both partitions. We should
not retain the old argument that a child is required to avoid whole-investigram replacement.
Children can still provide appropriate shared qualification and readable organization. State
category identity need not inherit a behavioral default-sampling limitation merely because the
initial behavior and identification were returned together. Original qualification is not
silently removed when an item is associated elsewhere.

Partition alone does not prevent reconsideration. Actual entity/result exposure, exact designated
membership and generating-query provenance determine the prototype's warnings. The more precise
policy remains an open decision, including partial delivery and the conservative effects of generating-evaluation exposure.

## 8. Composition and subsequent selection

The [composition probe](structured-account-example/composition/README.md) extends the children
scenario. Q4 receives selected P/S/R/B projections and supplies ordered-pattern entities plus
RESULT-Q4. It preserves the different setups and claimed resource lifetimes for both wrapper
orders. A later investigation can select C-BB as an entity or select a local relation/state
assignment within RESULT-Q4. The selector does not create a new identity or assert that the
scenario relation is the earlier W.

The earlier label “admission activity/decision flow” did not establish UML activity

## Identity and supersession remain separate

If later investigation discovers duplicate entities or conflation, preserve the original IDs
and content and express qualified correspondence. Association supersession does not automatically
merge entities, reinterpret an old ID, distribute claims among successors, or establish
transitive equivalence. Explicit entity supersession, warning scope, competing/challenged
identity claims and selection-origin fields remain design work. Cross-Session identity is out
of this first scope.

| Identity probe | Qualified finding | Organizational limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Separate discoveries S1/S2 | Same-thing correspondence with stated basis and limits | Preserve both IDs; matching names alone does not join them. |
| Proposed merge into S3 or one existing identity | Explicit successor proposal | Do not silently pool every old claim or erase original associations. |
| Conflated R split into category S and execution phase E | Explain the conflation and distinguish successor referents | Reassociate particular content only with qualified grounds; unresolved content remains attributable to R. |

A warning about use of a conflated entity may be needed even when the using investigation
never received its initial description. That is neither automatic association displacement
nor already covered by the current two propagation paths. Entity-use cause scope, challenge
and clearance remain unimplemented. A reconciliation investigation could examine selected
retained claims and return compatible scopes, correspondence, synthesis or unresolved
disagreement. It need not force agreement or introduce human pushback; the latter stays
deferred. No reconciliation Lens is delivered by the fixtures.

## What the example establishes

The exact payloads exercise references, association operations, result designation, qualified
entity context, immutable history, rare declaration-only contributions and two paths into
reconsideration. The existing state/sequence diagrams and their legends still regenerate.
They do not establish live investigation quality, a product API, universal result organization,
general reassessment, performance bounds, semantic identity or automatic reconciliation.
