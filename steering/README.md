# Steering

This directory holds human-maintained material for considering future directions, selecting work, and revisiting priorities. It is durable but non-permanent and non-governing: inclusion does not approve a plan, accept a decision, or authorize implementation.

Maintain these documents through separate human-directed discussion, not as part of plan drafting, promotion, or implementation. Report implications for steering separately rather than including proposed revisions in a planning package. Follow the [steering and notes conventions](../dev/process-conventions.md#durable-notes), including restrictions on links from canonical and historical documents, and any more specific editing boundaries in individual documents.

- [Upcoming work](upcoming-work.md): current selection, ordering, and decision points for strategy discussion.
- [Candidate capabilities](candidate-capabilities.md): possible capabilities and the questions worth considering before pursuing them.
- [Candidate UX](candidate-ux.md): possible investigation experiences and their unresolved design questions.
- [Design principles](design-principles.md): current design preferences and the reasoning behind them.

The [backlog](../docs/backlog.md) also records worthwhile work awaiting human selection and authorization. Unlike the documents here, agents may add backlog entries during planning or implementation under the [backlog guidance](../dev/workflow.md#backlog).
