## Upstream interpretive findings

**Cockatiel.** The initial summary gives useful invocation/classification/policy
boundaries but omits consequential callback-exception behavior. Explanation and
decomposition create more precise selectable responsibility accounts. Examination
adds sharp edges but overstates failure-listener exceptions always escaping: the
handled-result notification is inside the outer try/catch. The view-only evaluator
notices the restrictive wording instead of silently accepting it. The scoped
reference does not settle every consumer, EventEmitter, test or documentation
claim. No correction was submitted; retained summary reuse therefore preserves
its earlier omissions. The sequence adds comprehension while leaving a concrete
semantic qualification problem and considerable reading overhead.

**fsm-engine.** Explanation distinguishes handled-stay behavior from doing no
work, and decomposition separates pre-commit from post-commit failure. Examination
of the latter supplies qualified consequences rather than a replacement. The
source-informed assessor supports the principal dispatch, transition ordering,
fallback and no-rollback account, while noting the omitted qualification that
instance context is caller-supplied and not deeply copied. Timer-payload suspicion
remains qualified, not an observed runtime defect. Several detailed dependency,
documentation and test claims exceed the compact reference. A server-overload
failure during decomposition received the single protocol-authorized recovery;
the failed attempt and its unavailable usage remain in the record.

Neither of these upstream sequences submitted a correction. Their generic revision
footers do not demonstrate replacement or reconsideration behavior; that evidence
comes from deterministic tests and the separately attributed controlled cases.

**merge-anything.** The summary follows delegation and distinguishes runtime merging,
array policy and compile-time typing. Explanation supplies the selective recursion,
reference-sharing and descriptor-normalization details; examination adds source-level
access ordering, cycle/alias qualifications and the README's narrower description
of callback triggering. These deeper claims are preserved as interpretation, and
parts exceed the frozen reference's coverage. The initial summary overload received
a successful single recovery. Decomposition then failed with another overload and
was not repeated; the planned examination used the existing explanation as its
subject. Consequently this is an incomplete four-lens sequence, not a successful
decomposition. Exact failures are included in the role inputs and final evidence.
No correction was submitted in this upstream case either.

## Controlled correction findings

**Refined case.** The first live examination acquired source and interface-only
README, rejected the one-label precedence account and submitted an accepted
replacement for A with its own child. It did not separately correct A's original
child. Exact inspection retains A and that old child; repeated summary selects
the replacement's own composition. Y receives a direct cause and Z a transitive
cause from the correction. The reporting evaluation is exempt from its own cause.
Earlier views and records remain unchanged.

Clarification of Z supplies the correct multi-label consequences but reinterprets
“need not represent additional classifications” as a cautious warning about loss,
whereas the frozen setup intended it as an assurance that additional labels need
not be represented. This semantic ambiguity reduces discriminating power. No
fixture, qualification or prompt was adjusted in response, and the original
warning remains: clarification does not clear a citation cause.

Examination of Y attempted a further correction but failed submission validation.
It cited the correction record itself in root and correction evidence; correction
context is attributed through its reporting investigram, and correction IDs are
not eligible evidence records. Every handle resolved exactly. This is an
[evidence-contract failure](refined-numeric-m5/rejection-analysis.json), not absent
correction delivery or a reference-transport failure. The entire proposed result
was rejected, no replacement for Y was retained and no retry or repair was made.
The successful first correction does not erase this failure or establish robust
correction behavior across the full sequence.

**Conflict case.** Scripted B and C both correct A, with C initially selected by
recency while the alternatives remain explicitly unresolved. The live examination
of Z again reads the ambiguous wording as directionally correct and submits no
correction. Live clarification of older B does submit an accepted source-supported
replacement. That newer descendant becomes the primary for A across the older
branch; C remains an unresolved alternative. The source-informed assessor finds the evaluator understands these distinctions,
while identifying the source-detail view's missing actual excerpt as a material
presentation omission: it supplies a mapping and digest, not the source body.
This is consistent with the already-recorded source-navigation backlog concern.
The resulting whole-evaluation and complete-context exemptions are audited separately from earlier Y/Z warnings.
No semantic reconciliation or automatic warning clearance is claimed. Exact
historical inspections and retained redisplays preserve attribution and add no
inference. The live branch extension demonstrates a consequential selection case;
it does not turn the scripted disagreement into a naturally occurring model conflict.
