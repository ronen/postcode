human's diagnostic allowance (two used, eight remaining at freeze).

an evidenced plausibly transient communication failure, in the same shell with a
fresh dialogue. Authentication/configuration/structural failure, quota/spending
exhaustion or an unclassified error stops for diagnosis or human direction. No
repeat for refusal, truncation, invalid submission or execution-limit stop;
continue independent cases and mark dependent steps blocked. Preserve all failures.
Reauthorization pauses for the human. Reaching the administrative ceiling is a
pause for reporting, not milestone failure. No automatic inference retry exists.
