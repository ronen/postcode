ExecuteWrapper owns fresh success/failure emitters and exposes their subscription functions. invoke awaits the callback. A value rejected by resultFilter becomes a handled failure `{value}`; any other value becomes `{success}`. A thrown error matching errorFilter becomes `{error}`; a nonmatching error is reported as unhandled and rethrown. clone preserves the two predicates but creates new emitters.
