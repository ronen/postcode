States of ConsecutiveBreaker
  [Below threshold]  The persisted counter state is less than the instance's configured threshold.
  [Threshold reached]  The persisted counter state is greater than or equal to the configured threshold. This is a comparison category, not a latched open/broken mode: later failures still increment the counter.
  [Unordered numeric comparison]  Neither counter < threshold nor counter >= threshold is true, as can occur when the counter or threshold is NaN.
