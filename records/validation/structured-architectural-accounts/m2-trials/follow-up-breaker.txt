States of breaker
  [Below opening criterion]  The implementation's stored outcome history does not currently satisfy its Closed-circuit opening criterion.
  [Opening criterion satisfied]  The implementation's stored outcome history currently satisfies its Closed-circuit opening criterion.
Transitions
T1  Below opening criterion → Below opening criterion
      trigger: success(state) or failure(state) records an outcome
      guard: After the implementation-specific reset, eviction or window rotation and recording, the opening criterion remains unsatisfied.
      effect: Updates the implementation's persistent statistics. A failure returns false when the supplied circuit state is Closed, except that ConsecutiveBreaker ignores that supplied state; CountBreaker and SamplingBreaker return true for a failure supplied with any non-Closed state.
T2  Below opening criterion → Opening criterion satisfied
      trigger: success(state) or failure(state) records an outcome
      guard: After the implementation-specific reset, eviction or window rotation and recording, the opening criterion becomes satisfied.
      effect: Updates persistent statistics. A Closed-state failure reports that the circuit should open; CountBreaker and SamplingBreaker also report true for failures supplied with a non-Closed state regardless of the criterion.
T3  Opening criterion satisfied → Opening criterion satisfied
      trigger: success(state) or failure(state) records an outcome
      guard: The updated persistent statistics still satisfy the implementation-specific opening criterion.
      effect: Updates persistent statistics and applies the same failure return rules as the crossing transition.
T4  Opening criterion satisfied → Below opening criterion
      trigger: success(state) or failure(state) records an outcome
      guard: A reset or replacement/rotation of old samples causes the updated statistics no longer to satisfy the opening criterion.
      effect: Updates persistent statistics. ConsecutiveBreaker always reaches this category on success because it resets its counter to zero; the windowed implementations can drop below their criteria as old outcomes are replaced or discarded.
