# Audit Records

This directory preserves exploratory audits and supporting evidence for later planning and implementation. An audit records findings against a stated baseline; it does not approve a plan, authorize implementation, or establish that an implementation review gate has passed.

Collections have descriptive dated directories, an index, and identifiable subjects and reviewers. Preserve authored findings and their verification limits. Keep later synthesis and dispositions separate from the original findings. When relocating evidence, document mechanical link changes, original baselines, and any artifacts not retained.

Independent implementation-review handoffs, findings, and dispositions remain under [review records](../reviews/README.md) and follow the [independent review workflow](../../dev/review.md).
